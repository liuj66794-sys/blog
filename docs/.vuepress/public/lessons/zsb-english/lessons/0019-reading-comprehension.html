题型技巧 · 第二课

阅读理解 Reading Comprehension

对应教材上册 P212–P221 · 3 篇 15 题 30 分——先题后文圈关键词，金三句里找依据；题目分细节理解、词汇理解、是非判断、推理判断、主旨大意五类。

考点地图
  1. 阅读三步走：审清题目（圈关键词）→ 定位原文（金三句）→ 对比选项（同义替换、高度概括）
  2. 五类错误选项：无中生有 · 扩大或缩小范围 · 反客为主 · 极端错误 · 以假乱真
  3. 五类题型：细节理解 · 词汇理解（猜词七法）· 是非判断 · 推理判断 · 主旨大意
  4. 实战：题海 25 篇 121 题

第一部分 · 阅读三步走

  1. 审清题目：找出题目中的关键词并重点标记——动词、名词；数字、年份、地点；大写单词；观点对象、副词、形容词、否定词。若题干没有明显定位词，就根据题号顺序定位相应段落。
  2. 定位原文：根据定位词找到「金三句」——前一句 + 定位句 + 后一句。不能只截取孤立词语作答，要结合上下文理解。
  3. 对比选项：将选项与关键词所在位置的上下文逐项比对。若选项出现同义替换或对原文的高度概括，它可能是正确选项。
正确选项的两个条件 1. 定位准确：能够在「金三句」中找到依据。
2. 改写正确：实词能在原文中找到对应表达或同义词；限定语不能随意增减。课件列出的限定语：名词、形容词、冠词、形容词性物主代词、不定代词、定语从句、状语从句——尤其要注意条件状语从句。

第二部分 · 五类错误选项

类型特征对策
无中生有原文从未提到，但内容通常与文章主题相关应试阅读必须忠于原文：即使某项是常识或真理，原文没提到也不能选
扩大或缩小范围把 some 偷换成 all，或把特定对象扩大为全部对象文章中的限定语不能随意增减
反客为主原文只是客观描述，选项却人为加入主观态度读懂作者表达的观点，不读完后另行发表个人判断
极端错误把文章未出现的极端范围擅自加入选项警惕 only · unique · completely · entirely · wholly · always · never · every/any one · everything/anything 及形容词、副词最高级
以假乱真原文某词的词性或词形在选项中发生变化，往往伴随词义变化核对完整词形、词性和语义，不能因为「长得像」就视为同义

第三部分 · 五类题型与解题方法

题型一 · 细节理解题（教材 P213）

直接信息题常用 when、where、who、what、why、how 等特殊疑问词提问，答案通常是原文某句或短语的直接表述：
Where did the story happen? → The story took place in a small town.
间接信息题需要对文中信息进行加工、转换、归纳或合理推断：

  • 归纳并列信息：The new library has a large collection of books. It also provides comfortable reading areas and free Wi-Fi. → 特点＝藏书丰富 + 阅读区舒适 + 免费无线网络。
  • 识别同义替换：He is proficient in English. → He is good at English.

定位技巧：①关键词定位——圈出有标记意义的词或词组再回原文搜索（What was the main reason for the decline of the small local shops in the 1990s? → 定位 the rise of large supermarkets）；②同义定位——题干关键词和原文用词可能不同，但语义相同（policy ↔ regulation，beneficial to the majority of the citizens ↔ good for most citizens）。课件例题：

题型二 · 词汇理解题（教材 P214–216）

考查两类内容：一是判断超纲词的含义，二是判断熟词僻义或词语在特定语境中的含义。常见题干：The word “…” (in line…) most probably means _____. / From the passage, we can infer that the word “…” is _____.

猜词方法线索课件例句
1. 根据定义、解释或说明be / be called / call 等判断词、定语从句和标点符号常提供解释The herdsman, who looks after sheep, earns about 650 yuan a year.（定语从句解释 herdsman＝牧人）
2. 根据对比关系but / however / yet / otherwise / though 前后常有明显对比Though Tom’s face has been washed quite clean, his neck still remains grubby.（与 clean 对比＝肮脏的）
3. 根据因果关系because / since / as 提示原因；so / so…that… / such…that… 提示结果She wanted the hairdresser to trim her hair a bit because it was too long.（头发太长 → trim＝修剪）
4. 根据生活常识结合逻辑、生活经验和上下文判断Most of the roses are beginning to wither because of the cold.（寒冷对植物的影响 → wither＝枯萎）
5. 根据同等关系同一成分、同一范畴的词常由 and 或 or 连接At forty-two he was in his prime and always full of energy.（同等关系 → prime＝盛年时期）
6. 运用构词法（参考《前置讲义》P25）前缀改变词义：un-「不、非」、re-「再、又」；后缀决定词性：-ness、-able；合成词拆分推断unhappy 不开心的 · rewrite 重写 · happyness 幸福 · readable 可读的 · book+store＝bookstore 书店
7. 根据列举的事例（课件补充）后文列举同类事物You can take any of the periodicals: The World of English, Foreign Language Teaching in Schools, or English Learning.（列举的都是期刊 → periodicals＝期刊、杂志）

题型三 · 是非判断题（教材 P216–217）

题目给出若干陈述，要求根据文章判断其正确（True）、错误（False）或未提及（Not Mentioned）。审题时对 not true、be not mentioned / included、all the following except 等否定表达保持敏感。常见题干：Which of the following is not true? / The following statements are true EXCEPT _____.

解题关键：①将题干和各选项与原文相关细节逐项比较，特别注意同义转换；②可使用排除法，措辞过于绝对的选项应优先核查并排除。课件例题「智齿」：

On our 18th birthdays we might get a cake and a card, maybe a special gift. But there is an unwanted and sometimes painful present that many of us get at that time of life—wisdom teeth.

They usually come between the ages of 17 and 25 and there are usually four at the back of your mouth. They’re known as wisdom teeth because they arrive at a time when we might be beginning to feel wise.

Although, some studies have said that only a little more than half of people get their wisdom teeth at all.

For those that do, they can cause a lot of pain as they break through the gums and try to find a space in the corners of our mouths—and dentists may recommend removing them.

So if we don’t really need them, why do we have them?

Well, the answer that most experts give is that we may not need them now, but our ancestors did.

That’s because they ate tough, uncooked foods—things like meat, plants and nuts. So having wisdom teeth helped them grind the foods down to make them easier to eat.

Of course, now we cook many of these tougher foods to make them softer, so we don’t need to work so hard with our teeth.

And over time, our jaws have become smaller. There’s often still space for the wisdom teeth to grow in the jaw yet not quite enough for them to erupt in the mouth—and that’s often the reason for the pain.

Can we choose the cake and the card instead?

题型四 · 推理判断题（教材 P218–219）

考查对文章隐含或深层含义的理解，需要根据内容作合乎逻辑的推断，包括理解作者观点、判断态度，以及理解修辞、语气和言外之意。题干关键词：infer（推断）、indicate（象征、暗示）、imply / suggest（暗示）、conclude（得出结论）、assume（假定、设想）。

  • 细节推理题：根据人物行为、语言或事件细节，推断背后的原因、动机或结果。
  • 观点态度推理题：从用词、语气和论述方式推断作者或人物的态度（常见态度词见下表）。
  • 写作意图推理题：判断写作目的是介绍事物、解释现象、说服读者、呼吁行动等。选项常见动词：explain 解释、prove 证明、persuade 劝说、advise 劝告、comment 评论、praise 赞扬、criticize 批评、entertain 娱乐、demonstrate 举例说明、argue 论证、tell 讲述、analyze 分析。
  • 文章来源推理题：根据内容、风格和语言特点，判断可能来自报纸、杂志、网站、小说、学术论文等。常见题干：The passage is probably taken out of _____. / Where does this text probably come from?

常见题干：What is the author’s attitude towards _____? / What is the author’s opinion on _____? / The author’s tone in this passage is _____. / The purpose of the text is _____. / By mentioning…, the author aims to show that _____.

三条解题原则 1. 推理题考查「由已知推未知」；若题目明确要求推断，文章中原样直接陈述的内容通常不是所求推论。
2. 推理不是凭空猜测，必须能在文中找到事实、线索或理由。
3. 忠实于原文，不能用自己的观点替代作者观点，不能脱离文本主观臆断。

Many a young person tells me he wants to be a writer. I always encourage such people, but I also explain that there’s a big difference between being a writer and writing. In most cases these individuals are dreaming of wealth and fame, not the long hours alone at a typewriter. “You’ve got to want to write,” I say to them, “not want to be a writer.” The reality is that writing is a lonely, private and poor-paying affair; for every writer kissed by fortune there are thousands more whose longing is never rewarded. When I left a 20-year career in the U.S. Coast Guard to become a freelance writer, I had no prospects at all. What I did have was a friend who found for me a room in a New York apartment building. It didn’t even matter that it was cold and had no bathroom. I immediately bought a used manual typewriter and felt like a genuine writer.

题型五 · 主旨大意题（教材 P220–221）

文章未必直接给出主旨，需要从关键词和主干句中提炼、加工、概括。三种命题形式：

命题形式常见题干
Main idea 型What’s the main idea / point of the passage? · The passage is mainly about / mainly concerned about _____. · Which of the following best states the main idea of the passage?
Main purpose 型What’s the purpose of the passage? · The passage is meant to _____. · The purpose of the article is to _____. · In this passage the writer tries to tell us that _____. · The passage tells us that _____.
标题型The best title of the passage is _____. · Which of the following is the best title of the passage? · The most appropriate title of the passage is _____.

四步定主旨：

  1. 确定文章体裁：记叙文围绕人物经历展开（人物成长、情感表达或道理揭示）；说明文介绍事物特征、功能、原理或解释现象；议论文提出观点并用论据论证。
  2. 寻找主题句：首段的首句或尾句常直接提出主题；尾段结尾可能总结主旨、与开头呼应；少数主题句出现在中间段开头或结尾。如 Global warming is one of the most serious challenges facing our planet today.（首段主题句）；In conclusion, the key to solving the traffic problem lies in the joint efforts of the government, the public and the transportation departments.（尾段主题句）
  3. 概括段落大意：没有明显主题句时，先找每段关键信息和主要内容，再分析段落间逻辑关系，最后归纳核心思想。
  4. 分析文章结构：总—分（开头主题句即主旨）；总—分—总（首尾共同体现主旨）；分—总（重点看结尾总结）。
排除三类干扰项 以偏概全：只涉及部分内容——文章讨论多种动物的生活习性，选项只写一种动物；
过于宽泛：超出文章讨论范围——文章只讲某种特定植物的药用价值，选项泛指所有植物的作用；
无中生有：加入文章没有提及的内容。

In the wild, it often comes down to predator and prey—the hunter and the hunted. As you can imagine, most animals and plants want to stay alive. They have developed ways of adapting to severe habitats, and hiding or escaping from those who would like to eat them. So how do they do it?

One very helpful adaptation is called camouflage. You may have been surprised by an animal that was using camouflage in the past. It mixed itself with its environment so well that you nearly missed seeing it at all. Its coloring, markings, or other physical features are so similar to its habitat that you can look directly at it without seeing it at first. This is often good enough to fool a predator that is scanning an area to look for food. This helps prey to hide from its predator. But did you know that it often works the other way around, too? Predators can use camouflage to trap their prey. If a predator wants to eat a certain animal, and that animal cannot see it lying in wait, it can attack its prey unexpectedly, eating it before it even knows what is happening.

Another popular adaptation is mimicry. Mimicry is when an animal has markings or other physical characteristics that allow it to look like some other kind of animal or plant. If it can make its predators believe that it is something that preys on them, or would at least be difficult or painful to catch, its predator will often go off in search of an easier target.

Sometimes animals are able to survive when their habitat changes because they adapt to the new conditions. For example, birds that were used to nesting on high mountains or in tall trees have survived industrialization of their habitat by learning to nest in tall buildings. Raccoons could easily adapt to residential areas that have taken over their forest homes. They often help themselves to any food they can grab, whether it is in trash cans, or inside people’s homes!

易错清单

高频易错 1. 限定词不可丢：some / about / more than / in one way at least 一旦被换成 all / every / in every way，往往是扩大范围
2. 题目问 NOT / EXCEPT 时先圈否定词，再逐项回原文；练习二、八、九、十、十一、十二、十七都有此类题
3. 代词先找同数、同类的先行词：如练习十七的 them 指要父母购买的 products，不能只看距离最近的名词
4. 推断只走半步：从明示信息作最小延伸，不把愿望变成事实，也不补原文未说的因果
5. 词义看上下文功能：vulnerability → weakness、summit → highest point、lagging behind → fall behind，都可由后文解释或对比锁定

练习二 · 二维码

(1) It is impossible to ignore the popularity of Quick Response (QR) codes in China. These are square-shaped black-and-white symbols in WeChat or Alipay where QR codes are scanned to add new friends or make payment.

(2) The QR Code system originated in Japan. The engineer Masahiro Hara felt the limitation of the one-dimensional bar code in use and thus developed the QR code, which could carry data horizontally and vertically. QR codes could hold much more information than bar codes due to their extra dimension. In addition, QR codes have larger data capacity, smaller print-out size and higher resistance to damage.

(3) Though the technology of QR codes is complicated, we can easily create our own QR codes with the help of QR code generators, many of which are available online for free. The process can be fun when we customize the design according to our needs: adjust the color, add a logo or create social options. Dynamic QR codes allow users to edit data entered previously, whereas data in standard QR codes cannot be edited once printed. Do not limit a QR code to one mobile scanner: anyone can scan it with any reader.

(4) Meanwhile, we must also recognize the vulnerability of QR codes. Due to their easy access, criminals can steal simply by replacing the QR codes with their own, transferring cash to their own bank accounts. Hence, we should become more conscious of virtual-money transactions and take additional security measures.

练习三 · 社交媒体影响者

(1) It is estimated that about 40 percent of the world’s population use social media, and a large number of social media users look up to influencers to decide what to buy. These influencers are now playing an important role in achieving the goal of eliminating poverty in China.

(2) What is an influencer and how does a person become one? An influencer is a person who can influence the decisions of followers because of the relationship with the audience and his or her knowledge in a particular area.

(3) Influencers often have large followings of people who pay close attention to their views. They have the power to persuade people to buy things, and many companies ask powerful influencers to market their products.

(4) According to a report from China Daily, in an event targeted at helping poverty reduction, influencers attracted more than 930,000 views. More than 45,000 kilograms of agricultural products, at a total value of 40,000 yuan, were ordered within a few hours. Such events have encouraged more and more people to become influencers. Here are five tips on how to do it.

(6) Choose your medium and write an interesting bio. Most influencers these days are bloggers and micro-bloggers. The best way to connect with followers is to decide which medium to use, such as your own online blog, WeChat or TikTok. Then write an attention-grabbing bio that describes you and your areas of knowledge in an interesting and unique way.

练习四 · 策划晚宴

(1) A dinner party is often a pleasurable time for everyone but the host, so one important point to keep in mind is that “planning” is the key part of a successful dinner party.

(2) All too often hosts make the mistake of overexerting themselves, leaving little time to enjoy the evening. The following information can help make an enjoyable night for both you and your guests.

(3) Firstly, make a guest list so that you’ll know how many people to cook for. Let your guests know the date and time in advance, and remind them shortly before the party. Make a checklist of ingredients and drinks and buy them a couple of days beforehand. If you leave house-cleaning until party day, you may be more stressed than necessary. Keeping the menu simple does not mean it has to be boring. The night will be easier if you put dishes at the center of the table and let guests serve themselves.

(4) You can provide some music; its type will depend on the guests. The night will run much more smoothly with background music.

练习六 · 帽店老板的定价

(1) Most American people hate repeating the same work endlessly, especially those who live in West America. They try their best to make their life colorful and their work interesting. If they have to do the same regular work, they will try to do it in different ways. Repetition and monotony are what they cannot stand.

(3) One day a young tourist entered a hat shop and found a very nice hat that cost five dollars. He said to the shopkeeper, “If you agree to sell at a cheaper price, I’d like to buy two hundred hats of this style.” “No, you should pay me at least ten dollars each if you wish to get so many hats of the same style.”

(5) “Well,” said the shopkeeper, “If you buy only one hat of this style, I can make it easily. If you order two hundred hats of different styles, I can also manage the business without much difficulty. 练习八 · 教练眼中的迈克尔·乔丹

(1) Most people love Michael Jordan. He is the NBA’s No. 1 player who can run fast, jump high and shoot well. All these things are true, but there are other truths not so easily seen: his understanding of teamwork, his competitive spirit and his love for the game. It is these truths that form the deepest connection between Michael and me.

(2) After coaching him for eight seasons, I still wonder at Michael’s love for the game, even in practice games. He never takes a day off and takes nothing about his game for granted. When he first came to the NBA in 1984, his outside shooting was not perfect, so he practised hundreds of shots each day. In the end, he became a real three-point shooter.

(3) Playing outstanding defense did not come straight to him, either. He had to study his opponents and their usual moves, then learn the skills needed to stop them. He worked extremely hard to perfect his footwork and balance. Many young people think talent is all they need for success; Michael thinks otherwise. He knows his weaknesses and has turned them into strengths.

(4) While very competitive, Michael always respects other players, whether teammates or opponents. He often gives a hand to an opponent who falls down. He would never build himself up by tearing someone else down. Through his outstanding performance he has won love and respect from teammates and opponents.

(5) Every season, Michael makes himself available to groups of children with serious diseases. He puts them at ease, lets them laugh and enjoy basketball. When he retires, I hope young players like Grant Hill and Kobe Bryant will be influenced by his performance and fair play. Being Michael’s coach has been a great joy; more importantly, I consider him a friend.

练习九 · 麦当劳的全球化

(1) Since its founding in 1948, McDonald’s has grown from a family burger stand to a global fast-food chain, with more than 30,000 locations in 118 countries.

(2) With 58 million daily customers worldwide, McDonald’s is now so ubiquitous that The Economist publishes a global ranking of currencies’ purchasing power based on local McDonald’s prices, called the Big Mac Index. That does not mean every nation carries the same menu items: choices vary widely by location. Some Asian locations serve fried shrimp in a Big Mac roll, while McDonald’s in India serves no beef, relying on burgers made from vegetables, rice and beans.

(3) Not everyone has been happy to greet Ronald McDonald. Many see McDonald’s as a symbol of American economic and cultural chauvinism, and European nations in particular view American-style fast food as an insult to their national food. In 1999, French farmer José Bové and a group destroyed a McDonald’s under construction to protest globalization and “bad food.” The next year, a bomb exploded in a French McDonald’s, killing a 27-year-old employee. No one claimed responsibility.

练习十 · 长途飞行

(1) Air travel is an everyday experience these days because it is so fast. But long-distance flying can be very tiring, and the traveler begins to feel his brain is in one country while his body is still in another. In short, he hardly knows where he is.

(3) Doctors say that air travelers should go straight to bed on arrival after crossing a number of time zones. They should not work without rest. Airline pilots, however, do not need to do it. They often live by their own watches, paying no attention to local time. They can even have breakfast at midnight if necessary.

(4) Businessmen are not so lucky. After a long journey, they must either go straight to an important meeting or meet their boss to give a report. Even so, many businessmen still travel by air.

练习十一 · 吉姆·索普

(1) Jim Thorpe was a Native American. He was born in 1888 in an Indian Territory that is now Oklahoma. Like most Native American children then, he liked to fish, hunt, swim and play games outdoors. He was healthy and strong, but had very little formal education. In 1950, he was named the greatest American football player. He was also an Olympic gold-medal winner, but had many tragedies in his life.

(2) Jim had a twin brother who died when he was nine. By the time he was sixteen, his parents were also dead. He then went to a special school in Pennsylvania for Native American children. There he learned to read and write and began to play sports. Because he was poor, he left school for two years to earn money and played on a baseball team for only $15 a week. He soon returned to complete his education.

(3) In 1912, at age twenty-four, Thorpe joined the U.S. Olympic team and competed in the pentathlon and decathlon. The pentathlon has five track-and-field events; the decathlon has ten, including running, jumping and throwing contests.

(4) People thought it impossible for one athlete to compete in both events, so everyone was surprised when Thorpe won gold medals in both.

练习十二 · 为超重者设计的健身房

(2) Many people are self-conscious of their bodies and feel isolated in workout classes, especially if they are larger than most others. The fitness industry is finally paying attention. (4) Trainers recommend functional fitness as a practical goal, rather than six-pack abs. They often use text messages to stay in touch with customers.

(5) At these specialized gyms, trainers are often overweight themselves or working on their own weight goals, which can help people with anxiety caused by poor body image. Equipment is designed for larger people: wider seats, more cushioning, no mirrors and tinted windows for privacy.

练习十四 · NO-AD 的口碑营销

源题异常课件在 p70–73 仅列 1–4 题，没有第 5 题；此处按源文件保留，不补造题目。

(1) Is advertising really necessary? Billions of dollars are spent on it every year, so it must be important. After all, it is a busy world. You have to advertise to get people’s attention and sell products.

(2) Not every company thinks that way. Despite avoiding traditional advertising, the NO-AD company (“no-ad” stands for “not advertised”) has seen a steady increase in profits. Because its advertising plan is atypical, it saves money and keeps costs down, making products more affordable.

(3) NO-AD sells products by word of mouth. This happens when one person tells another about a good experience with a product or service; that person then tells a friend, family member or colleague, creating a chain of information.

(4) Advertisers typically say their product is the best, which can sound insincere or unconvincing. It is more believable to hear from a family member or friend who has used and liked it. Our loved ones’ opinions matter, so we often follow their product advice.

练习十五 · 工作有益健康

(1) Scientists find that hard-working people live longer than average men and women. Career women are healthier than housewives. Evidence shows that the jobless are in poorer health than jobholders. Whenever the unemployment rate increases by 1%, the death rate increases correspondingly by 2%. It all comes down to one point: work is helpful to health.

(2) Why is work good for health? It keeps people busy and away from loneliness. Research shows that people feel unhappy, worried and lonely when they have nothing to do; the happiest are those who are busy.

(4) Besides, work gives one a sense of fulfillment and achievement, value and status in society. When a writer finishes writing, a doctor successfully operates on a patient or a teacher sees students grow, they are happy beyond words.

(5) We can conclude that the more you work, the happier and healthier you will be. Let us work hard, study and live a happy, healthy life.

练习十七 · 包装也是广告

(1) Packaging is an important form of advertising. A package can motivate someone to buy a product. A child might ask for breakfast food in a box with a TV character, being more interested in the picture than the food. Pictures to color or cut out, games printed on a package, or small gifts inside a box also motivate children to buy products or ask parents for them.

(2) Some packages suggest a buyer will get something for nothing. Food sold in reusable containers is an example. Although a similar product in a plain container may cost less, people often prefer the reusable glass or dish because they believe the container is free. In fact, its cost is added to the product.

(3) Package size also motivates buyers. “Economy Size” suggests the large size offers the most product for the least money, but that is not always true. A buyer must know how the product is sold and the price of the basic unit.

(4) Information on the package should provide some answers, but buyers must remember that a package is often an advertisement. Its words and pictures do not tell the whole story; only the product inside can do that.

练习十八 · 国际体育竞赛

(1) Some people argue that pressure on international sportsmen and sportswomen kills the essence of sport and the pursuit of personal excellence. Children kick a football for fun. As they get older and play for school teams, they become competitive but still enjoy playing. An individual representing his country cannot afford to think about enjoyment; he must think only about winning and is responsible for the nation’s hopes, dreams and reputation.

(2) A good example is the FIFA World Cup. Football is the third most important sport in the world. Winning the World Cup is perhaps the summit of international sporting success. Mention Argentina and people will probably think of football. In a sense, winning the World Cup put Argentina on the map.

(4) So, am I arguing that international competition kills the idea of sport? Certainly not! Do the Argentinians really believe their nation is better in every way because eleven men were most skillful at football? Not really. 练习十九 · 免费音乐的商业模式

(1) It seems obvious that you do not give away your product for free, but indie rock group The Crimea did exactly that earlier this year. Its reasoning was that more people would download a free album than would pay for it.

(2) Therefore more people would hear its music, then pay for concerts and perhaps buy a T-shirt or other merchandise. Regular concerts for crowds of 200 or 300 could make more money than CD sales. Some people still want something physical, so the band would release CDs in shops too—but earning from music sales was not the top priority.

(3) The story illustrates creative thinking in the music business in response to dramatic changes in how people buy music. Digital music sales to computers, phones and MP3 players rose to $2 billion in 2006—almost 100 percent more than the previous year—yet overall record-company sales were down. People no longer bought CDs in anything like the old numbers.

(6) Bad news for record companies is the amount of illegally downloaded music. Piracy, usually cheaply copied CDs, has long been an issue, but the Internet allows music to be copied and distributed freely through file-sharing sites on a larger scale than ever.

练习二十 · 招聘面试新趋势

源题异常课件 p100 将第 2 题误标成「12.」；本课按逻辑题序记为第 2 题，题干与选项不变。

(1) Hiring may differ from one company to another, but the goal is the same: get the candidate who is the best fit. How should that person be chosen? Here are best practices used by industry leaders to attract the best talent—new trends to prepare for in a job interview.

(2) A typical interview is held in a conference room or office, making it easy for employer and candidate to act predictably. The problem is that afterward, employers often do not have a much better feel for the candidate than after reading the resume.

(3) Some employers create teams to hire new employees instead of having one administrator conduct the interview. They get team members’ opinions and, more importantly, can hire someone with whom current employees can get along.

(4) Employers also want candidates to provide new viewpoints. They consider the diversity of viewpoints a candidate can bring. Be ready to explain how your unique perspective adds value and helps solve problems creatively.

练习二十三 · 极简主义

(5) Extreme minimalism is not for everyone, but a mild form has recently gained popularity thanks to Marie Kondo’s best-selling book The Life-Changing Magic of Tidying Up. Kondo is known for tidying skills and believes one should own only things one truly loves.
