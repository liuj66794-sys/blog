数词和连词 Numerals & Conjunctions

对应教材上册 P75–P81 · 数词考确切/不确切数量与倍数句型；连词考四大关系 + 就近原则。

考点地图
  1. 数词：基数词（确切/不确切数量）、序数词、分数/小数/百分数、倍数
  2. 连词：并列连词（并列/转折/选择/因果四类）+ 从属连词总览（详见从句三课）

第一部分 · 数词

1. 基数词：确切 vs 不确切数量

口诀0–12 特殊记；13–19 加 teen（13、15、18 特殊记）；整十加 ty（20、30、40、50、80 特殊记）
类型规则例
确切数量hundred/thousand/million/billion 前有具体数词 → 用单数、不接 oftwo hundred；three thousand；11 million
不确切数量泛指 → 用复数 + of，前不能有具体数词（可被 many/several/a few 修饰）hundreds of people；tens of millions of

There are hundreds of people in the park.（好几百人）
There are three hundred people in the park.（三百人）

2. 序数词

口诀 123 特殊记，其余都加 th；8 去 t，9 去 e；ve 要用 f 替；见 y 变 ie，词尾再加 th
first / second / third · eighth · ninth · fifth / twelfth · twentieth
  1. 序数词前一般加 the：The first chair is Li Ping's.
  2. 表"又一、再一"可用 a/an（first 除外）：Try a second time and you'll succeed.
  3. 前有物主代词、名词所有格时不再用 the：My first class ✔
  4. 与基数词连用时序数词在前：the first three pages
  5. 修饰谓语动词时 the 省掉：The team ranks first in its country.

3. 分数、小数、百分数

分数：分子基数词、分母序数词；分子大于 1，分母加 s（8/9 → eight ninths；4/5 → four fifths）。分母是 2/4 用 half / quarter。也可用 in / out of：五分之八? eight in eight? 注意分子在前：five out of eight。
小数：点读 point，零读 zero/o，小数点后逐位读：53.16 → fifty-three point one six
百分数：基数词 + percent：100 percent。分数/百分数 + of + 名词作主语时，谓语与 of 后的名词一致：
Two thirds of the money was spent on food.

4. 倍数的四种句型

一倍 once，两倍 twice/double，三倍以上"基数词 + times"。

句型例句
倍数 + as + 原级 + asThis room is twice as big as that one.
倍数 + 比较级 + thanThis rope is four times longer than that one.
倍数 + the + size/length/width + ofThis avenue is twice the width of that one.
比较级 + by + 倍数This rope is longer than that one by four times.
口诀as 原级 as 连，than 前必有比较级，有 by 倍数在其后，the 后名词 of 现

第二部分 · 连词

并列连词连接对等成分（词+词、短语+短语、句+句）；从属连词引导三大从句（详见定语从句、名词性从句、状语从句三课）。

关系连词例句
并列andI like playing basketball, football and volleyball.
both...and...Both Jim and Mike are going to the Great Wall.（谓语复数）
neither...nor...Neither the teacher nor the students are in the classroom.（就近）
not only...but (also)...Not only Lily but also her parents are friendly.（就近）
转折butI love you but I don't believe you.
yetIt's a small car, yet it's very spacious.
while（对比）I love tea while my father loves coffee.
选择or（否则 or else / otherwise）Put the gun down, or else!
either...or...Either he could not come or he did not want to.
not...but...She is not a teacher but a student.
因果so（表结果）It was late, so I went home.
for（推断性原因，分句不能放句首）It must have rained last night, for it is wet all over.
不能连用but 和 although 不能在同一句连用（so 和 because 同理）：
✔ Although I am not good-looking, I am very gentle.
✔ I am not good-looking, but I am very gentle.
✘ Although I am not good-looking, but...
but/yet vs however but/yet 是连词，直接连两句；however/nevertheless 是副词，需句号/分号隔开再接逗号：He has a good job. However, he never has money.

易错清单

高频易错 1. two hundred ✔ / two hundreds ✘；hundreds of 前不能加具体数词
2. nine → ninth（去 e 加 th）；eight → eighth（去 t）
3. 序数词前有物主代词/名词所有格就不加 the
4. 分子大于 1 分母加 s：two thirds；of 后名词决定主谓一致
5. 倍数四句型要背熟
6. but 与 although、so 与 because 不能连用
7. 就近原则三兄弟：neither...nor / not only...but also / either...or；both...and 谓语用复数
8. however/nevertheless 是副词
9. for 表推断性原因，分句不能放句首
