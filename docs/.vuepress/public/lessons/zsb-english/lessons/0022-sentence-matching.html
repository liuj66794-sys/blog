题型技巧 · 第五课

文章句子匹配 Sentence Matching

5 题 × 2 分，共 10 分——先看话题、结构与设空形式，再圈选项中的逻辑词和实词，最后从逻辑、词汇照应和设空位置三条线索匹配。

考点地图 · 点击跳转
  1. 题型：5 题，每题 2 分，共 10 分（教材上册 P223-229）；考查文章整体内容、结构和上下文逻辑意义
  2. 步骤：第一步看话题 / 结构 / 设空形式，第二步圈选项中的逻辑词与实词（起承转合四大类信号词）
  3. 技巧：细节逻辑（因果 / 转折 / 例证 / 递进 / 平列）+ 词汇线索（代词 / 同义词 / 上下义词）+ 设空位置（段首 / 段尾）

考情分析（教材 P224；课件 p3）

考试分值：5 题 × 2 分 = 10 分。考纲要求：主要考查考生对文章的整体内容和结构以及上下文逻辑意义的理解和把握，侧重语言结构形式以及上下文的连贯手段。

解题步骤（教材 P224-225；课件 p4-8）

第一步 · 了解文章话题、结构和考查形式

  1. 观察文章话题：看大标题、小标题、主题句或第一段。
  2. 观察文章结构：辨认总分、总分总等结构；勾画祈使句或结构一致的句子。
  3. 观察设空处标点：空后是否为逗号？选项是半句还是完整句？先用句法形式排除无法衔接的选项。

第二步 · 看选项，画出逻辑词和实词

关系课件列出的信号词
起：时间关系起初、开始、第一：at first, first, firstly, in the first place, to start with, to begin with；第二、其次：second, secondly, then, afterwards, next, later；最后：at last, finally, last；其他：in the meantime（在此期间）、at the same time（与此同时）
承：并列与递进也：too, either, also, as well as, likewise（也、同样地，书面用语）；否则：or, otherwise；此外：besides, additionally, furthermore, moreover, in addition to, what's more；其他：not only...but also, neither...nor..., either...or..., both...and, similarly, equally, in the same way, that is to say, the same...as
转：转折与让步但是：but, however, yet, while, nevertheless, whereas；尽管、即使、虽然：although, though, even though, even if, despite, in spite of；正相反：instead of, rather than, on the other hand, on the contrary, by contrast；其他：unlike, unfortunately
合：因果关系因为：because, because of, for, since, as, thanks to, due to, in that；因此：so, therefore, thus, hence, as a result, consequently, accordingly；其他：so...that, for this reason

解题技巧（教材 P226-229；课件 p9-18）

线索一 · 细节逻辑（第 1–5 招）

1. 因果关系

前后句之间存在原因和结果，可能是前因后果，也可能是前果后因。常见标志有 as a result（结果）、thus / therefore（因此）、so / such...that...（如此……以至于……）等。

She insisted on organizing her class notes every day and reviewing her wrong questions in a timely manner after class throughout the semester. As a result, she achieved top grades in all subjects in the final exams.
她整个学期坚持每天整理课堂笔记，并在课后及时复习错题。因此，她在期末考试的所有科目中都取得了最高成绩。

2. 转折关系

后句对前句形成逆转逻辑时，设空处很可能需要转折句。

Although she spent weeks researching and preparing for the job interview, she still failed to get the offer because the company decided to hire an internal candidate at the last minute.
尽管她花了数周研究并准备这次求职面试，她仍未获得录用，因为公司在最后一刻决定聘用内部候选人。

3. 例证关系

前后句中的一句可能是为另一句举例。例证形式可以是例子或类比，其思维关系是从例子归纳观点，或从观点推到例子。

Developing effective reading habits is essential for enhancing one's language competence. For example, a student who insists on reading one English article every day and taking notes on new words and sentence patterns will gradually expand their vocabulary and improve their ability to understand complex texts.
养成有效的阅读习惯对提升语言能力至关重要。例如，坚持每天阅读一篇英语文章并记录生词和句型的学生，会逐渐扩大词汇量，提高理解复杂文本的能力。

4. 递进关系

后句对前句形成层进、顺承关系时，设空处很可能是递进句。

Regular physical exercise can significantly boost your physical health by strengthening your heart and improving blood circulation. Furthermore, it also plays a crucial role in enhancing mental well-being.
经常锻炼可以通过增强心脏功能、改善血液循环来显著促进身体健康。此外，它对改善心理健康也起着重要作用。

5. 平列关系

同一层面并列列举几个要点，常用顺序词 First / Second 等衔接。

To successfully host a memorable dinner party, you need to make thorough（充分的）preparations in advance. First, you should confirm the number of guests and inquire about their dietary preferences to ensure the menu meets everyone's needs. Second, you ought to clean and decorate your dining space properly.
要成功举办一场令人难忘的晚宴，你需要提前做好充分准备。首先，应确认宾客人数并询问他们的饮食偏好，确保菜单满足每个人的需要。其次，应妥善清洁并布置用餐空间。

线索二 · 词汇线索（第 6–8 招）

6. 代词

代词指代前文提及的名词或名词性短语。利用指代关系以及代词单复数差异，可以快速判断句子能否衔接。

She spent nearly a month making a well-designed study plan for her upcoming language test, and it has helped her arrange her daily learning tasks more systematically and efficiently.
她花了将近一个月，为即将到来的语言考试制订了一份精心设计的学习计划；这份计划帮助她更系统、更高效地安排每天的学习任务。

7. 同义词与近义词

上下文常重复使用同义词、近义词、近义表达或相同词汇，这是重要的匹配线索。

Mastering effective time management skills is extremely important for students who need to balance multiple courses and extracurricular activities. In fact, such skills are vital to help them make full use of every moment and enhance their overall learning efficiency significantly.
对需要兼顾多门课程和课外活动的学生来说，掌握有效的时间管理技能极其重要。事实上，这些技能是帮助他们充分利用每一分钟、显著提高整体学习效率的关键。

8. 上下义词与同一范畴词

上义词包含下义词，下义词是上义词的具体子集；同一范畴词也能形成语义照应。上义词与下义词不必字面重复，也能构成强照应。

She plans to buy some fresh fruits to make a healthy salad for lunch. Specifically, she intends to pick up a box of strawberries, a handful of blueberries and a big watermelon from the nearby grocery store（食品杂货店）.
她计划买些新鲜水果，午餐做一份健康沙拉。具体来说，她打算从附近的食品杂货店买一盒草莓、一把蓝莓和一个大西瓜。

上义词是 fresh fruits（新鲜水果），下义词是 strawberries（草莓）、blueberries（蓝莓）、watermelon（西瓜）。后者均属于前者的具体类别，这种上下义关系使前后句语义衔接更紧密。

线索三 · 设空位置（第 9–10 招）

9. 段首设空

  • 段首设空通常考查段落主题句。
  • 认真阅读后文，按段落一致性原则寻找同义词或相关词，推断主题句。
  • 重点阅读后文前一两句，锁定信号词，再在选项中寻找相关特征词。

10. 段尾设空

  • 所选答案可能引出下一段；若与前文关联不明显，应检查它能否衔接下一段开头。
  • 第一段段尾设空要判断此处是细节还是主题。第一段常提出全文主题；若段尾转出主题，常有转折信号词。
  • 普通段尾常是结论或概括句，应关注 therefore, as a result, thus, hence, in short, to sum up, to conclude, in a word 等结果、结论和总结信号词，并查找与前文的同义照应。

易错清单

高频易错 1. 不要只凭单个重复词选答案；必须同时验证逻辑关系、指代和句法形式
2. 段首优先找主题概括，段尾优先找总结、结果或承上启下
3. 代词必须能找到明确先行词，人称与单复数也要一致
4. 看到 however / although 等词先判断转折，看到 therefore / as a result 等词先判断因果
5. 上义词与下义词不必字面重复，也能构成强照应
6. 选项放回原文后，要再通读整段，确认内容与结构都连贯

课堂练习（2025 / 2024 真题）

以下两篇真题均按课件原题收录。原题为「从 A–E 五个句子中为 5 个设空选句」，此处转成逐空五选一：题干问该空应填回原文的哪一句，选项即原题 A–E 五句（保持原 5 个选项），解析给课件的判断依据（指代、复现、关键词对应）。课件原答案：2025 年为 16-B、17-C、18-E、19-D、20-A；2024 年为 16-D、17-C、18-A、19-B、20-E。

真题一 · 2025 年（课件 p19-22）

Modern life can be stressful, but small changes in your daily routine may help improve your mood. Sometimes a short escape can make you feel better — this is supported by research showing that physical separation from daily stressors correlates with reduced anxiety levels. 16 ______ If you stay away longer, it might even help reduce half of your stress or sadness.

The research team, led by psychologist Tim Bush, conducted a study on mental well-being involving over 5,000 participants. 17 ______ The results were surprising. 18 ______ Similarly, non-social interactions like pet ownership showed benefits parallel to social activities in improving moods.

The study also examined how people cope with stress. 19 ______ Then he gradually returned to normal activities after being alone for some time. This behavior highlighted an important psychological principle: your actions directly influence your emotions.

20 ______ If you choose positive activities — whether social, physical, or even pet-related — you'll likely feel happier. So, next time you're upset, try going out for a while or spending time with a pet — it might really help!

真题二 · 2024 年（课件 p23-27）

It is well established that eating vegetables is good for us. Why, then, do some people like them while others don't? 16 ______ According to the research, people who eat vegetables and show an obvious unpleasant feeling for them can influence others.

A team of scientists set out to investigate how the facial expressions that people make as they eat affect a person watching them. They asked more than 200 college students to watch videos of other people eating raw broccoli（花椰菜）. 17 ______ They would smile, seem neither happy nor sad, or look annoyed or upset by what they were eating.

The study found that when participants watched someone else eating broccoli with a strong dislike, they began to like broccoli less. 18 ______ When someone had a positive facial expression while eating broccoli, people watching them did not end up liking broccoli more.

19 ______ This means that if children see their parents or brothers and sisters not enjoying certain food, including vegetables, they might not want to eat them either.

The team hopes to understand more about how the behavior of adults influences children's enjoyment of food. 20 ______
