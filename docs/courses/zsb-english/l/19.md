题型技巧 · 第二课

对应教材上册 P212–P221 · 3 篇 15 题 30 分——先题后文圈关键词，金三句里找依据；题目分细节理解、词汇理解、是非判断、推理判断、主旨大意五类。

::: info 考点地图

1. **阅读三步走**：审清题目（圈关键词）→ 定位原文（金三句）→ 对比选项（同义替换、高度概括）
2. **五类错误选项**：无中生有 · 扩大或缩小范围 · 反客为主 · 极端错误 · 以假乱真
3. **五类题型**：细节理解 · 词汇理解（猜词七法）· 是非判断 · 推理判断 · 主旨大意
4. **实战**：题海 25 篇 121 题

:::

## 第一部分 · 阅读三步走

1. **审清题目**：找出题目中的关键词并重点标记——动词、名词；数字、年份、地点；大写单词；观点对象、副词、形容词、否定词。若题干没有明显定位词，就根据题号顺序定位相应段落。
2. **定位原文**：根据定位词找到「金三句」——**前一句 + 定位句 + 后一句**。不能只截取孤立词语作答，要结合上下文理解。
3. **对比选项**：将选项与关键词所在位置的上下文逐项比对。若选项出现**同义替换**或对原文的**高度概括**，它可能是正确选项。

::: info

正确选项的两个条件
1. **定位准确**：能够在「金三句」中找到依据。

2. **改写正确**：实词能在原文中找到对应表达或同义词；限定语不能随意增减。课件列出的限定语：名词、形容词、冠词、形容词性物主代词、不定代词、定语从句、状语从句——尤其要注意条件状语从句。

:::

## 第二部分 · 五类错误选项

| 类型 | 特征 | 对策 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **无中生有** | 原文从未提到，但内容通常与文章主题相关 | 应试阅读必须忠于原文：即使某项是常识或真理，原文没提到也不能选 |
| **扩大或缩小范围** | 把 some 偷换成 all，或把特定对象扩大为全部对象 | 文章中的限定语不能随意增减 |
| **反客为主** | 原文只是客观描述，选项却人为加入主观态度 | 读懂作者表达的观点，不读完后另行发表个人判断 |
| **极端错误** | 把文章未出现的极端范围擅自加入选项 | 警惕 only · unique · completely · entirely · wholly · always · never · every/any one · everything/anything 及形容词、副词最高级 |
| **以假乱真** | 原文某词的词性或词形在选项中发生变化，往往伴随词义变化 | 核对完整词形、词性和语义，不能因为「长得像」就视为同义 |

下面 4 道课件例题，每题对应一类错误选项（课件原答案）：

**【无中生有】原文：Tom went to the store yesterday. He looked at various items on the shelves. After some time, he bought a bottle of milk.
题目：What did Tom do at the store?（汤姆在商店里做了什么？）**

- A. He bought a bottle of juice.（他买了一瓶果汁。）
- B. He thought the store was too crowded and decided to buy milk quickly.（他觉得商店太拥挤，于是决定赶快买牛奶。）
- C. He bought a bottle of milk.（他买了一瓶牛奶。）
- D. He looked at fruits only.（他只看了水果。）

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：C（He bought a bottle of milk.（他买了一瓶牛奶。））** —— 原文直接写明 bought a bottle of milk；B 增加「商店拥挤」和「赶快买」的原因，属于无中生有。

:::

**【扩大或缩小范围】原文：Some experts believe that the new policy will have a positive impact on small- and medium-sized enterprises.
题目：Which of the following is true according to the passage?（根据文章，以下哪项正确？）**

- A. Experts believe that the new policy will have a positive impact on all enterprises.
- B. Some experts think the new policy benefits small- and medium-sized enterprises.
- C. No experts believe the new policy is good for small- and medium-sized enterprises.
- D. Some experts doubt the new policy’s impact on small- and medium-sized enterprises.

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（Some experts think the new policy benefits small- and medium-sized enterprises.）** —— Some experts、small- and medium-sized enterprises 均与原文范围一致；A 把中小企业扩大为全部企业。

:::

**【反客为主 · 课件第 53 题】原文：Bob’s wife Sandy has a full-time job, and he really prefers her bringing home extra income. Yet he also feels uneasy and troubled. “She puts me off. First she makes dinner, then the kids need something, or a friend calls her. She talks with her sister, her girlfriends, her workmates, but not with me. I sometimes do feel abandoned and annoyed.”
题目：Which of the following statements about Bob and Sandy is true?（以下关于鲍勃和桑迪的陈述，哪项正确？）**

- A. Bob doesn’t wish Sandy to do a full-time job.
- B. Sandy is too busy to have time talking with Bob.
- C. Sandy puts Bob off since there’s no common topic between them anymore.
- D. Sandy feels equal to Bob now and no longer loves him as before.

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（Sandy is too busy to have time talking with Bob.）** —— 原文列举了桑迪做饭、照顾孩子、接朋友电话以及与亲友和同事交流等忙碌情形；C、D 擅自补入「没有共同话题」「不再爱他」等主观原因。

:::

**【极端错误 · 课件第 15 题】原文：Knowing the psychology of the game isn’t a sure ticket to winning. But it can help. Ken “Whitey” Watson is one of the world’s best rock, paper, scissors players. He says winning takes skill. “You have to size up your opponent.” Luck and chance play a part, but “you have to be prepared and have a strategy,” he says. “Part of my strategy is being confident.”
题目：What is Ken Watson’s view on the game?（肯·沃森对这个游戏有什么看法？）**

- A. Being relaxed is a sure ticket to winning.
- B. Being confident can increase the chance to win.
- C. Your chance of winning wholly depends on luck.
- D. Your chance of winning relates to the size of your opponent.

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（Being confident can increase the chance to win.）** —— being confident 是策略之一，能够增加获胜机会；A 的 sure、C 的 wholly 把原文的有限作用绝对化，D 把 size up（判断、估量）误解为「体型大小」。

:::

::: info

以假乱真 · 图示类比课件用多张仿冒品牌图作视觉类比：包装、配色或字母非常相似，并不意味着商品相同（图中可见仿「旺仔」、仿奥利奥及 adibas 等错拼标识）。做题时也要核对完整词形、词性和语义，不能只凭外观相近选择。

:::

转折前后课件 p12 用「原来的你 / 现在的你」的对照图提醒：转折前后信息可能发生变化，不能把自己的印象代替转折后的文本事实。

## 第三部分 · 五类题型与解题方法

### 题型一 · 细节理解题（教材 P213）

**直接信息题**常用 when、where、who、what、why、how 等特殊疑问词提问，答案通常是原文某句或短语的直接表述：

Where did the story happen? &rarr; The story took place **in a small town**.

**间接信息题**需要对文中信息进行加工、转换、归纳或合理推断：

- 归纳并列信息：The new library has a large collection of books. It also provides comfortable reading areas and free Wi-Fi. &rarr; 特点＝藏书丰富 + 阅读区舒适 + 免费无线网络。
- 识别同义替换：He is **proficient in** English. &rarr; He is **good at** English.

**定位技巧**：①**关键词定位**——圈出有标记意义的词或词组再回原文搜索（What was the main reason for the decline of the small local shops in the 1990s? &rarr; 定位 the rise of large supermarkets）；②**同义定位**——题干关键词和原文用词可能不同，但语义相同（policy &harr; regulation，beneficial to the majority of the citizens &harr; good for most citizens）。课件例题：

**原文：The first study found that among more than 350 Americans, increased reading of literary fiction when growing up was connected with greater “psychological richness.” … In the second study, the researchers said that among around 2,200 university students, those who read literary fiction were less likely to simply accept authority.
题目：According to the passage, university students who read literary fiction would ______.（根据文章，阅读文学小说的大学生会怎样？）**

- A. not easily accept authority（不会轻易接受权威）
- B. probably accept authority（可能接受权威）
- C. certainly not accept authority（肯定不接受权威）
- D. simply accept authority（轻易接受权威）

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：A（not easily accept authority（不会轻易接受权威））** —— 原文 were less likely to simply accept authority 与 A 同义；C 的 certainly 把「可能性较低」极端化。

:::

### 题型二 · 词汇理解题（教材 P214–216）

考查两类内容：一是判断**超纲词**的含义，二是判断**熟词僻义**或词语在特定语境中的含义。常见题干：The word “…” (in line…) most probably means _____. / From the passage, we can infer that the word “…” is _____.

| 猜词方法 | 线索 | 课件例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. 根据定义、解释或说明 | be / be called / call 等判断词、定语从句和标点符号常提供解释 | The herdsman, **who looks after sheep**, earns about 650 yuan a year.（定语从句解释 herdsman＝牧人） |
| 2. 根据对比关系 | but / however / yet / otherwise / though 前后常有明显对比 | Though Tom’s face has been washed quite **clean**, his neck still remains **grubby**.（与 clean 对比＝肮脏的） |
| 3. 根据因果关系 | because / since / as 提示原因；so / so…that… / such…that… 提示结果 | She wanted the hairdresser to **trim** her hair a bit **because it was too long**.（头发太长 → trim＝修剪） |
| 4. 根据生活常识 | 结合逻辑、生活经验和上下文判断 | Most of the roses are beginning to **wither** because of the cold.（寒冷对植物的影响 → wither＝枯萎） |
| 5. 根据同等关系 | 同一成分、同一范畴的词常由 and 或 or 连接 | At forty-two he was **in his prime** and always **full of energy**.（同等关系 → prime＝盛年时期） |
| 6. 运用构词法（参考《前置讲义》P25） | 前缀改变词义：un-「不、非」、re-「再、又」；后缀决定词性：-ness、-able；合成词拆分推断 | **un**happy 不开心的 · **re**write 重写 · happy**ness** 幸福 · read**able** 可读的 · book+store＝bookstore 书店 |
| 7. 根据列举的事例（课件补充） | 后文列举同类事物 | You can take any of the **periodicals**: *The World of English*, *Foreign Language Teaching in Schools*, or *English Learning*.（列举的都是期刊 → periodicals＝期刊、杂志） |

两道课件补充例题：

**原文：The schools are reluctant to take off—even teachers with tickets for the England-Argentina game had trouble getting the day off.
题目：The underlined word “reluctant” in the passage may probably mean _____.（文中画线词 reluctant 最可能是什么意思？）**

- A. kind（友善的）
- B. unwilling（不愿意的）
- C. free（自由的；空闲的）
- D. careless（粗心的）

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（unwilling（不愿意的））** —— 破折号后的「连持票教师都很难请到假」解释了学校不愿放假。

:::

**原文：Another popular adaptation is mimicry. Mimicry is when an animal has markings or other physical characteristics that allow it to look like some other kind of animal or plant.…
题目：Which of the following could best explain the word “mimicry” in paragraph 3?（以下哪项最能解释第 3 段中的 mimicry？）**

- A. An animal’s physical characteristics don’t look like some other kind of animal or plant.
- B. An animal’s physical characteristics look like some other kind of animal or plant.
- C. An animal’s physical characteristics look like the same kind of animal or plant.
- D. A plant’s physical characteristics look like some other kind of animal or plant.

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（An animal’s physical characteristics look like some other kind of animal or plant.）** —— 定义句直接说明：动物的身体特征使它看起来像另一种动物或植物。

:::

### 题型三 · 是非判断题（教材 P216–217）

题目给出若干陈述，要求根据文章判断其正确（True）、错误（False）或未提及（Not Mentioned）。审题时对 not true、be not mentioned / included、all the following except 等否定表达保持敏感。常见题干：Which of the following is not true? / The following statements are true EXCEPT _____.

**解题关键**：①将题干和各选项与原文相关细节逐项比较，特别注意同义转换；②可使用排除法，措辞过于绝对的选项应优先核查并排除。课件例题「智齿」：

On our 18th birthdays we might get a cake and a card, maybe a special gift. But there is an unwanted and sometimes painful present that many of us get at that time of life—wisdom teeth.

They usually come between the ages of 17 and 25 and there are usually four at the back of your mouth. They’re known as wisdom teeth because they arrive at a time when we might be beginning to feel wise.

Although, some studies have said that only a little more than half of people get their wisdom teeth at all.

For those that do, they can cause a lot of pain as they break through the gums and try to find a space in the corners of our mouths—and dentists may recommend removing them.

So if we don’t really need them, why do we have them?

Well, the answer that most experts give is that we may not need them now, but our ancestors did.

That’s because they ate tough, uncooked foods—things like meat, plants and nuts. So having wisdom teeth helped them grind the foods down to make them easier to eat.

Of course, now we cook many of these tougher foods to make them softer, so we don’t need to work so hard with our teeth.

And over time, our jaws have become smaller. There’s often still space for the wisdom teeth to grow in the jaw yet not quite enough for them to erupt in the mouth—and that’s often the reason for the pain.

Can we choose the cake and the card instead?

**Which of the following is NOT true?（以下哪项不正确？）**

- A. Usually wisdom teeth erupt through the gums in the corners of our mouths.（智齿通常在口腔角落穿过牙龈萌出。）
- B. Our ancestors’ jaws were bigger than ours.（祖先的下颌比我们的更大。）
- C. Our teeth do not need to work hard now because our jaws have become smaller.（如今牙齿不必费力，是因为我们的下颌变小了。）
- D. Our ancestors needed wisdom teeth to chew hard foods.（祖先需要智齿来咀嚼坚硬食物。）

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：C（Our teeth do not need to work hard now because our jaws have become smaller.（如今牙齿不必费力，是因为我们的下颌变小了。））** —— 如今牙齿不必费力，是因为较硬食物经过烹煮变软；下颌变小解释的是智齿萌出空间不足和疼痛。

:::

::: details 本篇中文译文

在我们 18 岁生日时，我们可能会收到蛋糕和贺卡，也许还有一份特别的礼物。但在人生的这个阶段，许多人还会收到一份并不想要、而且有时很疼的“礼物”——智齿。

智齿通常在 17 至 25 岁之间长出，口腔后部一般有四颗。它们被称为智齿，是因为它们出现时，我们也许正开始变得有智慧。

不过，一些研究指出，实际上只有略多于一半的人会长智齿。

对于会长智齿的人来说，智齿穿过牙龈并试图在口腔角落寻找空间时，可能造成剧痛；牙医可能会建议将其拔除。

那么，如果我们并不真正需要智齿，为什么还会有它们呢？

大多数专家给出的答案是：我们现在也许不需要，但祖先需要。

这是因为祖先吃坚硬且未经烹煮的食物，如肉、植物和坚果。智齿能帮助他们把食物磨碎，使食物更容易食用。

当然，现在我们会把许多较硬的食物煮软，因此牙齿不必那么费力工作。

随着时间推移，我们的下颌变小了。下颌里通常仍有空间让智齿生长，却没有足够空间让它在口腔中萌出，这往往就是疼痛的原因。

我们能不能只选蛋糕和贺卡呢？

:::

### 题型四 · 推理判断题（教材 P218–219）

考查对文章**隐含或深层含义**的理解，需要根据内容作合乎逻辑的推断，包括理解作者观点、判断态度，以及理解修辞、语气和言外之意。题干关键词：infer（推断）、indicate（象征、暗示）、imply / suggest（暗示）、conclude（得出结论）、assume（假定、设想）。

- **细节推理题**：根据人物行为、语言或事件细节，推断背后的原因、动机或结果。
- **观点态度推理题**：从用词、语气和论述方式推断作者或人物的态度（常见态度词见下表）。
- **写作意图推理题**：判断写作目的是介绍事物、解释现象、说服读者、呼吁行动等。选项常见动词：explain 解释、prove 证明、persuade 劝说、advise 劝告、comment 评论、praise 赞扬、criticize 批评、entertain 娱乐、demonstrate 举例说明、argue 论证、tell 讲述、analyze 分析。
- **文章来源推理题**：根据内容、风格和语言特点，判断可能来自报纸、杂志、网站、小说、学术论文等。常见题干：The passage is probably taken out of _____. / Where does this text probably come from?

| 英文 | 中文 | 英文 | 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| neutral | 中立的 | sympathetic | 同情的 |
| satisfied | 满意的 | friendly | 友好的 |
| enthusiastic | 热情的 | subjective | 主观的 |
| objective | 客观的 | matter-of-fact | 实事求是的 |
| pessimistic | 悲观的 | optimistic | 乐观的 |
| critical | 批评的 | doubtful | 怀疑的 |
| hostile | 敌对的 | indifferent | 冷淡的 |
| disappointed | 失望的 |   |   |

常见题干：What is the author’s attitude towards _____? / What is the author’s opinion on _____? / The author’s tone in this passage is _____. / The purpose of the text is _____. / By mentioning…, the author aims to show that _____.

::: info

三条解题原则
1. 推理题考查「由已知推未知」；若题目明确要求推断，文章中原样直接陈述的内容通常不是所求推论。

2. 推理不是凭空猜测，必须能在文中找到事实、线索或理由。

3. 忠实于原文，不能用自己的观点替代作者观点，不能脱离文本主观臆断。

:::

Many a young person tells me he wants to be a writer. I always encourage such people, but I also explain that there’s a big difference between being a writer and writing. In most cases these individuals are dreaming of wealth and fame, not the long hours alone at a typewriter. “You’ve got to want to write,” I say to them, “not want to be a writer.” The reality is that writing is a lonely, private and poor-paying affair; for every writer kissed by fortune there are thousands more whose longing is never rewarded. When I left a 20-year career in the U.S. Coast Guard to become a freelance writer, I had no prospects at all. What I did have was a friend who found for me a room in a New York apartment building. It didn’t even matter that it was cold and had no bathroom. I immediately bought a used manual typewriter and felt like a genuine writer.

**What can be concluded from the passage?（从文章可以得出什么结论？）**

- A. Writers’ success depends on luck rather than on effort.
- B. Genuine writers often find their work interesting and rewarding.
- C. Famous writers usually live in poverty.
- D. The chances for a writer to become successful are small.

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：D（The chances for a writer to become successful are small.）** —— for every writer kissed by fortune there are thousands more whose longing is never rewarded，表明成功者只是极少数；A 否定努力作用，B 把低报酬说成「回报丰厚」，C 把普通未成功写作者偷换成著名作家。

::: details 本篇中文译文

### 题型五 · 主旨大意题（教材 P220–221）

文章未必直接给出主旨，需要从关键词和主干句中提炼、加工、概括。三种命题形式：

| 命题形式 | 常见题干 |
| --- | --- |
| **Main idea 型** | What’s the main idea / point of the passage? · The passage is mainly about / mainly concerned about _____. · Which of the following best states the main idea of the passage? |
| **Main purpose 型** | What’s the purpose of the passage? · The passage is meant to _____. · The purpose of the article is to _____. · In this passage the writer tries to tell us that _____. · The passage tells us that _____. |
| **标题型** | The best title of the passage is _____. · Which of the following is the best title of the passage? · The most appropriate title of the passage is _____. |

**四步定主旨**：

1. **确定文章体裁**：记叙文围绕人物经历展开（人物成长、情感表达或道理揭示）；说明文介绍事物特征、功能、原理或解释现象；议论文提出观点并用论据论证。
2. **寻找主题句**：首段的首句或尾句常直接提出主题；尾段结尾可能总结主旨、与开头呼应；少数主题句出现在中间段开头或结尾。如 Global warming is one of the most serious challenges facing our planet today.（首段主题句）；In conclusion, the key to solving the traffic problem lies in the joint efforts of the government, the public and the transportation departments.（尾段主题句）
3. **概括段落大意**：没有明显主题句时，先找每段关键信息和主要内容，再分析段落间逻辑关系，最后归纳核心思想。
4. **分析文章结构**：总—分（开头主题句即主旨）；总—分—总（首尾共同体现主旨）；分—总（重点看结尾总结）。

::: danger 易错

排除三类干扰项
**以偏概全**：只涉及部分内容——文章讨论多种动物的生活习性，选项只写一种动物；

**过于宽泛**：超出文章讨论范围——文章只讲某种特定植物的药用价值，选项泛指所有植物的作用；

**无中生有**：加入文章没有提及的内容。

In the wild, it often comes down to predator and prey—the hunter and the hunted. As you can imagine, most animals and plants want to stay alive. They have developed ways of adapting to severe habitats, and hiding or escaping from those who would like to eat them. So how do they do it?

One very helpful adaptation is called camouflage. You may have been surprised by an animal that was using camouflage in the past. It mixed itself with its environment so well that you nearly missed seeing it at all. Its coloring, markings, or other physical features are so similar to its habitat that you can look directly at it without seeing it at first. This is often good enough to fool a predator that is scanning an area to look for food. This helps prey to hide from its predator. But did you know that it often works the other way around, too? Predators can use camouflage to trap their prey. If a predator wants to eat a certain animal, and that animal cannot see it lying in wait, it can attack its prey unexpectedly, eating it before it even knows what is happening.

Another popular adaptation is mimicry. Mimicry is when an animal has markings or other physical characteristics that allow it to look like some other kind of animal or plant. If it can make its predators believe that it is something that preys on them, or would at least be difficult or painful to catch, its predator will often go off in search of an easier target.

Sometimes animals are able to survive when their habitat changes because they adapt to the new conditions. For example, birds that were used to nesting on high mountains or in tall trees have survived industrialization of their habitat by learning to nest in tall buildings. Raccoons could easily adapt to residential areas that have taken over their forest homes. They often help themselves to any food they can grab, whether it is in trash cans, or inside people’s homes!

**What is the main idea of the text?（本文的主旨是什么？）**

- A. Animals adapt to their habitat in order to survive.
- B. Plants adapt to their habitat in order to survive.
- C. Animals and plants adapt to their habitat in order to survive.
- D. Both animals and plants use camouflage to mix themselves with the environment.

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：C（Animals and plants adapt to their habitat in order to survive.）** —— 首段总述 most animals and plants 为适应环境而发展出生存方式，后文分别说明伪装、拟态和适应新环境；A、B 以偏概全，D 将一种适应方式扩大为全部。

在野外，生存往往归结为捕食者与猎物——猎人与被猎者之间的较量。可以想象，大多数动物和植物都想活下去。它们已经形成适应恶劣栖息地、躲藏或逃离捕食者的方法。那么，它们是怎么做到的呢？

一种很有用的适应方式叫作伪装。你过去也许曾被一只正在伪装的动物吓一跳：它与环境融为一体，以至于你几乎完全没看到它。它的颜色、斑纹或其他身体特征与栖息地如此相似，你即使直视它，起初也可能看不见。这往往足以骗过正在搜寻食物的捕食者，帮助猎物躲开捕食者。不过，你知道这种方式也常常反过来起作用吗？捕食者也能利用伪装伏击猎物。捕食者若想吃某种动物，而那只动物看不见潜伏中的捕食者，捕食者就能出其不意地发动攻击，在猎物还不知道发生什么时就将其吃掉。

另一种常见的适应方式是拟态。拟态是指动物具有某些斑纹或其他身体特征，使它看起来像另一种动物或植物。如果它能让捕食者相信自己会捕食对方，或者至少难以捕捉、抓捕时会使对方受伤，那么捕食者通常会离开，寻找更容易的目标。

有时，栖息地改变后，动物能够通过适应新条件生存下来。例如，原本习惯在高山或高树上筑巢的鸟类学会在高楼上筑巢，从而在栖息地工业化后幸存。森林家园被住宅区取代后，浣熊也能轻易适应；它们会随手取用能抓到的任何食物，无论食物在垃圾桶里还是在人们家中！

想象走进一套几乎空无一物的公寓：只有简单桌子和折叠沙发，没有装饰，衣柜里的衣服一只手就能数完，像牢房胜过住宅，让人以为住户买不起更多东西。其实屋主是主动限制物品数量的极简主义者。极简主义通过大幅减少财物来对抗消费社会；实践程度虽不同，他们普遍认为减少多余物品能使生活更充实。财物少了，清洁、惦记缺少什么或追赶潮流所花的时间也少了，从而有更多时间运动、旅行、陪伴所爱之人。极端极简并非人人适合，但近藤麻理惠的畅销书让温和版本流行起来。她强调极简不是一味丢弃，而是找出真正想留下的东西，在个人快乐与财物之间取得平衡，为幸福腾出空间。

:::

### 练习二十四 · 合租伙伴与语言

(1) When I studied English at a training center in Washington, D.C. in 1998, I shared a house with two young Americans, Jim and Steve. Jim studied French and Steve Chinese at the Foreign Service Institute. We shared much in common but thought and acted differently. Steve was interested in speaking Chinese and used every opportunity to talk with me in his very English-like pronunciation. I was touched by his diligence. I wanted to practise English too, so we often spoke simultaneously in the other’s mother tongue.

Jim, fresh from college and the youngest, went out all the time. We met only at breakfast around a small kitchen table. Steve ate Chinese fast food such as dumplings or noodles; I ate bread and boiled eggs. Jim often drank only tea because his fridge was empty—he was too busy dating pretty French girls to shop. I offered him food, but Steve said in poor Chinese that I need not: it was Jim’s own fault and served him right. Although learning Chinese, Steve still held American values.

(3) Two years later I returned to China, but we three still keep in touch. Jim works at a travel agency in Paris and married one of the girls; he now enjoys breakfast with his wife. Steve wants to work in China, and I introduced him to the dean of my university’s Overseas Section. The dean is interested in whether Steve can teach Chinese to overseas students. I sent Steve the message and am sure he would accept, though I hope he improves his pronunciation—or all the students would speak with his terrible accent!

**Which statement is TRUE?（哪项正确？）**

- A. All three were language majors（三人都是语言专业学生）
- B. Steve and Jim were more alike in character（史蒂夫与吉姆性格更相像）
- C. The author did not enjoy talking with Steve（作者不喜欢与史蒂夫交谈）
- D. Their living condition was rather poor（他们居住条件很差）

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：A（All three were language majors（三人都是语言专业学生））** —— 定位 p117：作者学英语，吉姆学法语，史蒂夫学中文，三人都在学语言。

:::

**What is the author’s opinion of Steve?（作者怎样看史蒂夫？）**

- A. He was very hardworking（他非常勤奋）
- B. He enjoyed cooking Chinese food（他喜欢做中餐）
- C. He enjoyed shopping more than Jim（他比吉姆更爱购物）
- D. His Chinese accent was pure（他的中文口音很纯正）

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：A（He was very hardworking（他非常勤奋））** —— 定位 p118：作者被史蒂夫的 diligence 感动，说明他勤奋。

:::

**Which is TRUE about Jim?（关于吉姆，哪项正确？）**

- A. Dating girls took much of his time（与女孩约会占用他很多时间）
- B. He had a French way of making friends（他以法国方式交友）
- C. He learned French in order to date Paris girls（他为和巴黎女孩约会而学法语）
- D. He liked doing housework（他喜欢做家务）

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：A（Dating girls took much of his time（与女孩约会占用他很多时间））** —— 定位 p119：他 too busy dating … to do any shopping。

:::

**The last sentence of the first paragraph implies that _____.（第一段末句暗示什么？）**

- A. Steve did not like helping others（史蒂夫不喜欢助人）
- B. Americans eat only their own food（美国人只吃本国食物）
- C. Americans and Chinese differ in values（美国人与中国人的价值观不同）
- D. Steve wanted Jim to shop for himself（史蒂夫想让吉姆自己购物）

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：C（Americans and Chinese differ in values（美国人与中国人的价值观不同））** —— 定位 p120：史蒂夫虽学中文，仍坚持美国价值观；这体现中美价值观差异。

:::

**From the last paragraph, we learn that _____.（从末段可知什么？）**

- A. Jim is a very good husband（吉姆是好丈夫）
- B. Steve enjoys teaching Chinese（史蒂夫喜欢教中文）
- C. The author works for overseas students（作者为留学生工作）
- D. The three friends still keep in touch（三位朋友仍保持联系）

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：D（The three friends still keep in touch（三位朋友仍保持联系））** —— 定位 p121：末段首句直接说 The three of us still keep in touch。

:::

::: details 本篇中文译文

1998 年，“我”在华盛顿特区一所培训中心学英语，与美国青年吉姆和史蒂夫合租。吉姆学法语，史蒂夫学中文；三人有共同点，想法和行为也各不相同。史蒂夫勤奋，抓住一切机会用带浓重英语口音的中文和“我”交谈；“我”也想练英语，于是两人常同时说对方的母语。刚毕业且最年轻的吉姆总在外约会，三人只在早餐相见。他忙着和法国女孩约会，无暇购物，冰箱空空，常只喝茶。“我”想分食物给他，史蒂夫却认为那是吉姆自己的错，活该如此，体现了其美国价值观。两年后“我”回国，三人仍联系：吉姆在巴黎旅行社工作并已结婚；史蒂夫想来中国工作，“我”把他介绍给大学海外部主任，可能让他教留学生中文，不过他还得改善发音。

:::

### 练习二十五 · 太空中的吃与睡

(2) Food for space has to be packed in special ways. Some goes into tubes that a person can squeeze into the mouth. Bite-sized cookies are packed in plastic.

(3) There is a good reason for covering each bite. Plastic keeps food pieces from traveling in the spaceship. On Earth, tiny pieces fall to the floor, but without gravity in a spaceship they move around and can get into a person’s eyes or the instruments. If an instrument is blocked, astronauts may have trouble getting safely home.

(4) On longer trips, astronauts must sleep. An astronaut can fasten himself to a seat with a seat belt or sleep in a fixed sleeping bag under the seat. He must put his hands under the belt when sleeping because he is afraid of touching a control that is not supposed to be touched until later.

**Why cover each bite of food in space?（为什么在太空中包住每一口食物？）**

- A. Small pieces would fall to the floor（碎屑会落到地板）
- B. Weightless pieces might travel around and cause trouble（失重碎屑会漂浮并惹麻烦）
- C. There is not enough food for long trips（长途食物不足）
- D. Astronauts do not want to waste food（宇航员不想浪费食物）

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（Weightless pieces might travel around and cause trouble（失重碎屑会漂浮并惹麻烦））** —— 定位 p122：失重碎屑会漂动并进入眼睛或仪器。

:::

**In a spaceship, astronauts can _____.（在飞船中，宇航员可以怎样？）**

- A. walk as on Earth（像在地球上一样走路）
- B. eat nothing because it is dangerous（因危险而什么也不吃）
- C. control the ship while sleeping（睡觉时控制飞船）
- D. not litter small things, or they may cause trouble（不可让小东西散落，否则会惹麻烦）

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：D（not litter small things, or they may cause trouble（不可让小东西散落，否则会惹麻烦））** —— 定位 p123：散落的小东西可能堵住仪器，影响安全返航。

:::

**How is space food packed?（太空食品如何包装？）**

- A. Tiny food pieces are put on the floor（小食物碎片放地板上）
- B. Some food is put into tubes（部分食物装入管中）
- C. Bite-sized cookies are packed in plastic（一口大小的饼干用塑料包装）
- D. Both B and C（B、C 两项）

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：D（Both B and C（B、C 两项））** —— 定位 p124：食物既有管装，也有塑料包装的一口饼干。

:::

**Why does an astronaut put his hands under the belt while sleeping?（宇航员睡觉时为什么把手放在带子下？）**

- A. It is comfortable（这样舒服）
- B. He does not want to touch controls（不想误碰控制装置）
- C. Instruments break easily（仪器容易损坏）
- D. He fears the seat will move（害怕座位移动）

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（He does not want to touch controls（不想误碰控制装置））** —— 定位 p125：把手压在带下是为防止误碰控制装置。

:::

**What is the best title?（最佳标题是什么？）**

- A. Eating and Sleeping in Space（太空中的吃与睡）
- B. How Astronauts Eat in Space（宇航员怎样在太空进食）
- C. Food for Space（太空食品）
- D. How Astronauts Sleep in Space（宇航员怎样在太空睡觉）

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：A（Eating and Sleeping in Space（太空中的吃与睡））** —— 文章同时介绍太空中的食品包装与睡眠固定方式。

:::

::: details 本篇中文译文

太空食品必须用特殊方式包装：有些装在可挤入口中的管子里，一口大小的饼干则用塑料包装。每一口食物都要包好是有原因的：地球上的小碎屑会落到地板上，飞船里没有重力把它们拉下去，它们会四处飘动，可能进入人的眼睛或仪器；若仪器被堵塞，宇航员可能无法安全回家。长途太空旅行中，宇航员也要睡觉。他可以用安全带把自己固定在座位上，也可睡在固定于座位下方的睡袋里。入睡时必须把手放在安全带下面，以免误碰尚不该操作的控制装置。

:::
