I am writing to express my sincere gratitude to you for your generous help in preparing for our class party.

As the one responsible for the party, I felt stressed about the heavy preparation work at first. Luckily, you offered me great support without hesitation. You spent plenty of spare time helping us decorate the classroom and plan the party process carefully. Your patience and enthusiasm made the busy preparation much easier and smoother.

Thanks to your selfless help, our class party turned out to be a perfect success and won wide praise from all classmates. I really appreciate your kindness and reliable help. It is such a warm thing to have a helpful friend like you. Thank you again for everything you have done for me!

Yours sincerely,
Chen Xi

**解析**　两篇范文均说明张月提供的具体帮助、晚会成功结果并表达真诚感谢。
