题型技巧 · 第四课

题海 15 篇 · 每篇 5 空——把句子放回原文，考的是句间逻辑、段落结构、代词指代、词汇复现和上下文衔接。

::: info 考点地图

1. **题型**：15 篇题海练习（题海 P78–P90），每篇 5 空，从 5 个备选句中选句还原原文
2. **考什么**：句间逻辑、段落结构、代词指代、词汇复现、上下文衔接
3. **怎么练**：先选项 → 判空位 → 找指代 → 看逻辑 → 双向验证 → 全文复核

:::

## 题型概览

本课件为题海练习，共 **15 篇、每篇 5 空**（75 空）。源课件未写明本题型分值及方法论，下列解题步骤为笔记整理补充；所有练习原文、选项均按课件保留，课件未附答案，答案与解析为笔记整理补充。

## 解题步骤（五步）

1. **先读选项**：圈出人称代词、指示代词、连接词、重复词和主题词。
2. **再看空位功能**：段首多承上启下或概括本段；段中多解释、举例、转折或递进；段尾多总结或导向下文。
3. **双向验证**：既检查所选句能否承接前一句，也检查能否自然引出后一句。
4. **利用硬线索**：代词必须有先行词；however/but 前后转折；therefore/as a result 前因后果；编号小标题要保持结构平行。
5. **代入复核**：五项全部代入后通读全文，确认逻辑、语法和指代都唯一成立。

## 易错清单

::: danger 易错

高频陷阱
1. 只看某个重复词，不看逻辑方向

2. 选项与前句相连，却无法接后句

3. 忽略代词的单复数和指代对象

4. 把「具体例子」误放到需要「总起句」的段首

:::

Finally, you never know what wonderful idea might strike while your mind has moved slowly away. **___5___**.

The reason why any of us take any risks at all might have to do with early humans. Risk-takers were better at hunting, fighting, or exploring. **___2___**. As the quality of risk-taking was passed from one generation to the next, humans ended up with a sense of adventure and a tolerance for risk.

So why aren't we all jumping out of airplanes then? Well, even 200,000 years ago, too much risk-taking could get one killed. **___5___**. For the risk-seekers a part of the brain related to pleasure becomes active, while for the rest of us, a part of the brain related to fear becomes active.

### 练习八 · Levi Strauss and Blue Jeans

For over one hundred and fifty years, Americans of all social classes have worn blue jeans. **___1___**. Whether they are worn for work or for fashion today, Strauss' invention continues to be popular not only among Americans but also among people around the world.

Levi Strauss was born in Germany in 1829. **___2___**. He grew up in Kentucky before moving to New York in 1847. Before becoming an American citizen and moving to the West in 1853, Strauss worked in his brother's dry goods business. This gave him a chance to produce his famous invention. After the gold rush of 1849, Strauss decided to move to the West to seek his fortune.

Strauss did not want to be a person who searched an area for minerals. Instead, he knew he could make a good living by selling supplies to the miners. At first, he planned to sell sewing supplies and cloth. **___3___**. When he heard miners complaining that their clothes were easily broken or that they usually tore their pockets during mining, he decided to use a special fabric to make pants for the miners. These pants proved so popular that he quickly ran out of materials to make more.

In 1873, Strauss received a letter from a Jewish tailor named Jacob Davis who had invented a process of connecting pockets with copper rivets. This made the pants last a long time. Because Davis did not have the money to patent his idea, he offered to share it with Strauss if Strauss would agree to pay for the patent. **___4___**.

The business has been growing ever since and Levi Strauss' company is now one of the largest clothing companies in the world. By the time Strauss died in 1902, he had made a great contribution to American fashion. **___5___**.
