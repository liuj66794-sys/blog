语法专题 · 第四课

对应教材上册 P55–P68 · 介词表示名词、代词与句中其它词的关系。13 组介词辨析是考试重头戏。

::: info 考点地图

1. **分类与三要素** —— 简单/复合/短语/分词介词；介词后接什么、何时省略
2. **11 个常见介词** —— by、for、of、against、at、from、on、over、under、with、as
3. **13 组介词辨析**（考试重头戏）—— above/over/on、across/through、besides/except、in front of 等

:::

## 一、介词三要素

::: info

三要素
1. 介词后 + **名词 / 动名词 doing / 宾格**：

I am looking forward to **your reply**.（介+n.）期待你的回复。

She is afraid of **swimming**.（介+doing）她害怕游泳。

2. **地点副词前省略介词**：go **home**（不说 to home）

3. 介词**不可单独使用**

:::

## 二、11 个常见介词核心用法

| 介词 | 核心用法 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **by** | 在旁边；靠、通过（方法）；不迟于；被（被动语态） | stand **by** the window；make a living **by** selling...；return **by** Monday；written **by** an American writer |
| **for** | 为了；向往；支持（↔against）；因为；对于；持续一段时间 | prepare **for** the exam；famous **for** hot springs；wait **for** two hours |
| **of** | 所属（B of A = A 的 B）；of + great + 抽象名词 = very + 形容词 | the secret **of** success；It is **of great value**. = very valuable |
| **against** | 倚、靠；反对（↔for）；以……为背景 | his back **against** the wall；Flowers look better **against** green leaves. |
| **at** | 在（小地点）；在（时刻/年龄）；以（速度）；朝、向 | arrive **at** the airport；run **at** 120 mph；Don't laugh **at** me! |
| **from** | 来自；由……制成（看不出原料）；使免受 | a letter **from** her mother；Wine is made **from** rice. |
| **on** | 在表面上；关于；具体某一天 | **on** the desk；**on** May 15th；**on** Tuesday |
| **over** | 悬在正上方；到处；在……期间；越过 | fly **over** the woods；all **over** the country；jump **over** the wall |
| **under** | 在下面（↔over）；少于、未满 | Children **under** 6 are admitted free. |
| **with** | 和一起；带有；用（工具器官）；随着；with + n. + 宾补 | a country **with** a long history；see **with** our eyes；**With** the development of... |
| **as** | 作为、当作 | Don't treat me **as** a child. / **As for** me,... 至于我 |

前置课补充：off 离开/关闭（step off the train / turn off the lights）· about 关于 · to 到达/目的 · in 里面（in 2025 / in June / in summer）· out of 外面

::: danger 易错

辨析：be made of / from
**of 看得出原料**：The kite is made **of** paper.

**from 看不出原料**：The paper is made **from** wood.

:::

::: danger 易错

辨析：on / in the wall、tree
**on** the wall 挂在墙**表面** / **in** the wall 嵌在墙**里面**（如窗户）

**on** the tree **长**在树上（树叶果实）/ **in** the tree **落**在树上（外来物，如猫）

:::

## 三、13 组介词辨析（重头戏）

### 1. 在上面：above / over / on

| 词 | 区别 | 反义词 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| above | 相对水平高度，不一定正上方 | below |
| over | 垂直**正上方** | under |
| on | 与表面**接触** | beneath |

### 2. 穿过：across / over / through / past

**across** 表面穿过（walk across the road）· **over** 上方越过（jump over the wall）· **through** 内部穿过（through the forest）· **past** 从旁经过（walk past the shop）

### 3. 之间：between / among

**between...and...** 两者之间 · **among** 三者及以上中间

### 4–5. at / on / in 表地点与时间

::: info

时间口诀
**大于一天要用 in**（in May / in 2025 / in summer）；**等于一天要用 on**（on Sunday / on May 15th）；**小于一天要用 at**（at 7 o'clock）

**三长**（in the morning / afternoon / evening）**两短**（at night / at noon）特殊记

:::

::: danger 易错

注意**on + 修饰词 + morning**：on a cold morning；on Sunday morning

:::

### 6. 方法手段：by / with / in / through

| 介词 | 用法 | 例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| by | 交通工具、通信方式（**单数名词前不加冠词**） | by air / by car / by post |
| with | 有形工具、身体器官 | see **with** our eyes |
| in | 语言、材料 | in English / in blue ink / in cash |
| through | 抽象途径 | master English **through** more practice |

### 7. 除了：besides / except / except for / but / apart from

| 词 | 区别 |
| --- | --- |
| besides | 除……之外**（还）**——宾语包括在内 |
| except | 除……之外——宾语**排除在外**（同类之间） |
| except for | 整体肯定 + 局部否定（非同类） |
| but | 常配 nobody / none / nothing / all / everyone 等 |
| apart from | 可代替 besides / except for |

Everyone is present **except** Helen. / The composition is good **except for** a few spelling mistakes.

### 8. by the end of / at the end of / in the end

| 短语 | 含义 | 时态搭配 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| by the end of | 到……为止（仅时间） | **过去完成时或将来时** |
| at the end of | 在……尽头（可指地点和时间） | 一般过去时/将来时 |
| in the end | 最终 = finally，单用 | —— |

### 9. in front of / in the front of

**in the front of** 在……**前部**（范围内）：He is in the front of the car.（坐在前排）

**in front of** 在……**前面**（范围外）：His wife is in front of the car!（车外的前面）

### 10. by / before（时间点）

**by** = "不迟于某时，**包括**某时"，口诀"到哪哪完"：将来时间配将来时，过去时间配**过去完成时**：

I **had learned** more than 2000 words **by** the end of last term.

**before** 在某时之前，**不包括**某时。

### 11. like / as

**like** 表相似："像……一样"（He looks like his father.）；**as** 表身份、职业："作为"（He works **as** a waiter.）

### 12. because / because of

**because 连词接完整句子**；**because of 介词短语接名词/动名词**。同组：due to、thanks to。

### 13. of one's own / on one's own

**of one's own** 属于自己的（We have a library of our own.）；**on one's own** 独自地（She does the work on her own.）

## 易错清单

::: danger 易错

高频易错
1. on the tree 长在树上 / in the tree 落在树上；on the wall 挂表面 / in the wall 嵌墙内

2. be made of 看得出原料 / be made from 看不出

3. by + 交通工具单数不加冠词（by taxi ✔ / by a taxi ✘）

4. besides 加 / except 减 / except for 修细节 / but 配 no-all-every 系列

5. by the end of + 过去完成时或将来时；in the end 单用

6. in front of 范围外 / in the front of 范围内

7. on + 修饰词 + morning；但 in the morning、at night

8. of + great + 抽象名词 = very + 形容词

9. because 接句子 / because of 接名词

10. like 表相似 / as 表身份

:::

## 课堂练习

**What do you usually do ____ Sunday?**

- A. in
- B. on
- C. at
- D. for

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（on）** —— 等于一天（星期）用 on。

:::

**She was born ____ 2005.**

- A. in
- B. on
- C. at
- D. for

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：A（in）** —— 大于一天（年份）用 in。

:::

**He is good ____ playing football.**

- A. in
- B. at
- C. on
- D. for

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（at）** —— 固定搭配 be good at doing。

:::

**Mike does his exercises ______ seven ______ the evening.**

- A. on; to
- B. at; in
- C. by; of
- D. at; on

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（at; in）** —— 具体时刻 at seven；in the evening（三长之一）。

:::

**There is a cat ______ the tree.**

- A. in
- B. on
- C. at
- D. under

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：A（in）** —— 猫不是长在树上的，"落在树上"用 in the tree。

:::

**The bank is ______ the post office. They’re next to each other.**

- A. in front of
- B. behind
- C. beside
- D. across

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：C（beside）** —— next to each other（相邻）→ beside 在旁边。

:::

**There is a bridge ______ the river.**

- A. over
- B. under
- C. above
- D. across

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：A（over）** —— 桥横跨在河的正上方用 over（above 不强调跨越，across 是表面穿过）。

:::

**The car is parked ______ the house.**

- A. in the front of
- B. in front of
- C. on
- D. at

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（in front of）** —— 车停在房子外面的前面，范围外用 in front of；in the front of 指范围内部的前部。

:::
