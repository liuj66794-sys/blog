语法专题 · 第三课

对应教材上册 P19–P29 · 代词代替名词避免重复。七大类中，**不定代词辨析是考试重头戏**。

::: info 考点地图

1. 人称代词（主格/宾格、使用顺序、it 的 7 种用法）
2. 物主代词（形容词性 vs 名词性）· 反身代词（固定搭配）
3. 指示代词（this/that、电话用语、比较级中的 that/those）· 疑问代词
4. **不定代词**：it/one/that 系列 · another/other 系列 · each/every · either/neither · no one/none · all/both · few/little
5. 相互代词 each other / one another

:::

## 一、人称代词

| 人称 | 主格 | 宾格 | 形物代 | 名物代 | 反身代词 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 我 / 我们 | I / we | me / us | my / our | mine / ours | myself / ourselves |
| 你 / 你们 | you | you | your | yours | yourself / yourselves |
| 他 / 她 / 它 | he / she / it | him / her / it | his / her / its | his / hers / its | himself / herself / itself |
| 他们 | they | them | their | theirs | themselves |

::: info

口诀
主宾格：**动词前用主格；动词介词后用宾格；You、It 主宾同；其他主宾须分清**

排序：**单数人称二三一；复数人称一二三**（You, Tom and I... / We, you and they...）

物主代词：**两种代词形不同，添个 s 形变名；his、its 不用变，my 变 mine 要谨记**

反身代词：**一二形代第三宾，加上 -self 变反身**

:::

### 形物代 vs 名物代

形容词性物主代词 = 形容词，只作**定语**：We showed them some of **our** photos.

名词性物主代词 = "形物代 + 名词"，可作主语、宾语、表语，**后面不可再加名词**：**My** hair is very thin. **Yours** is much thicker.

### it 的 7 种用法

1. 指上文提到的事物：The company was losing money and **it** had to let people go.
2. 指不知性别的婴儿：She hopes **it** will be a girl.
3. 指时间、距离、天气：**It**'s ten o'clock. / **It** is raining.
4. 指已发生的事件：Stop **it**!
5. 识别、分辨人（电话常用）：**It**'s your mother on the phone.
6. **强调句**：It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who + 其余：**It** was three weeks later **that** he heard the news.
7. It is + adj. + for sb. + to do sth. 句型

## 二、反身代词的用法

1. 作宾语，译"……自己"：Help **yourself** to some fish, kids. / She taught **herself** English.
2. 作同位语，"亲自、本人"：My mom **herself** saw the terrible accident.
3. 固定搭配：**by oneself** 独自 · **seat oneself** 就座 · **enjoy oneself** 玩得开心 · **help oneself** 自取

## 三、指示代词

this/these 指**近**，that/those 指**远**。两个考试高频场景：

::: info

电话用语
——Hello, who is **that** speaking?

——**This** is Mary speaking.（介绍自己用 This is，不用 I am）

:::

::: danger 易错

比较级中 that / those
that 代替上文的**单数**比较对象，those 代替**复数**：

The weather of Beijing is much colder than **that** of Shanghai in winter.

The books in this bookstore are nicer than **those** in that store.

:::

## 四、疑问代词

| 词 | 用法 | 例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| who | 谁，作主语、宾语、表语 | Who gave you that book? |
| whom | 谁，作动词/介词宾语 | Whom did you go with? |
| whose | 谁的 | Whose book is it? |
| what | 可提问职业 | What is your father?（你父亲是做什么的） |
| which | 强调**选择范围** | Which do you prefer, milk or tea? |

## 五、不定代词（考试重头戏）

### 1. it / one / ones / the one / that / those

| 词 | 用法 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| it | **同类同物** | I have a pen. **It** is very expensive. |
| one / ones | = a + 单数（泛指同类中一个） | I need a pen. I can buy **one** in the shop. |
| the one / the ones | = the + 名词（特指） | **The one** on my foot is green. |
| that / those | **同类不同物**，特指 | The water in the cup is hotter than **that** in the bottle. |

::: danger 易错

两个高频规则
① 后面有 **of 短语**时，多用 that/those：The population of China is larger than **that** of Japan.

② 有**前置修饰语**时，只能用 one（ones）：I prefer the small books to the large **ones**.

:::

### 2. another / other / others / the other / the others

| 词 | 含义 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| other | 其他的 + 可数复数 | other students |
| the other | **两者中另一个** | One is red, **the other** is blue. |
| another | **三者及以上**中另一个（泛指） | Show me **another** one. |
| others | 另一些（泛指） | Some are playing, and **others** are jumping rope. |
| the others | 余下的**全部** | Some are from Beijing, **the others** are from Shanghai. |

::: info

加分句型**another + 数量 + 复数名词 = 数量 + more + 复数名词**（"又/再几个"）：

I will stay here for **another three weeks** (= three more weeks).

:::

### 3. each / every

| 维度 | each | every |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 强调 | **个体**独立性 | **整体**共同性 |
| 数量 | ≥2 | ≥3 |
| of 搭配 | each of + n. ✔ | **不可接 of**，只能 every + n. |
| 特殊搭配 | 不配 almost / not | 可配 **almost、nearly、not** |

::: danger 易错

判断对错She holds a flower in **every** hand. ✘ —— 人只有两只手，every 须用于三者以上，应用 **each** hand。

:::

### 4. either / neither

either = 两者中**任意一个**（either...or...）；neither = **两者都不**（neither...nor...）。

作主语时谓语**单数**：**Either is** okay for me. / **Neither knows** the answer.

either of / neither of + 复数作主语，谓语**常用单数**：**Neither of** the students **has** finished.

### 5. no one / none / nothing

| 项目 | no one | none | nothing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 指代 | 仅指人 | 人或物（**需明确范围**） | 仅指物 |
| 接 of | ✘ | **✔ 可接 of** | ✘ |
| 谓语 | 单数 | 可单可复 | 单数 |

### 6. all / both

**all** = 三个及以上都（反义 none）；**both** = 两者都（反义 neither），谓语用**复数**。all 指人用复数，指事物整体/抽象概念可单数。

of 后是宾格代词时 of **不可省**；of 后是名词时可省。

### 7. few / a few / little / a little

::: info

口诀
**few，little 表否定，前面加 a 变肯定；

可数名词复数前，用（a）few 记心间；

不可数名词来报到，其前要用（a）little。**

:::

## 易错清单

::: danger 易错

高频易错
1. it **同类同物**，that/those **同类异物**；后有 of 短语用 that/those，有前置修饰语只能用 one（ones）

2. the other（两者另一个）vs another（三者以上）vs the others（其余全部）vs others（另一些泛指）

3. every ≥3、each ≥2；every 不接 of；every 可配 almost/nearly/not，each 不行

4. neither/either of + 复数作主语，谓语常用**单数**

5. no one / nothing 不接 of；none 可接 of，动词可单可复

6. both 谓语用复数

7. few/a few 修饰可数复数，little/a little 修饰不可数；加 a 变肯定

8. another + 数量 + 复数 = 数量 + more + 复数

9. 反身代词固定搭配：by oneself、enjoy oneself、help oneself、seat oneself

:::

## 课堂练习

**—Which of the two T-shirts would you like to choose?
—____. I don’t like their styles.**

- A. Either
- B. Both
- C. None
- D. Neither

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：D（Neither）** —— 两件 T 恤都不喜欢用 Neither（两者都不）；None 需三者以上。

:::

**—Have you got ____ ready for the sports meeting?
—Not yet. We still have ____ to do.**

- A. anything; nothing
- B. something; everything
- C. everything; something
- D. everything; nothing

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：C（everything; something）** —— "一切"都准备好了吗用 everything；还没好，"还有一些事要做"用 something。

:::

**Is ____ necessary to take off our shoes when we enter the lab?**

- A. everyone
- B. this
- C. her
- D. it

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：D（it）** —— it 作形式主语，真正主语是 to take off our shoes。

:::

**His camera is more expensive than ____.**

- A. hers
- B. her
- C. it
- D. its

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：A（hers）** —— 与 his camera 比较，用名词性物主代词 hers（= her camera）。

:::

**Although he’s wealthy, he spends ____ on clothes.**

- A. little
- B. few
- C. a little
- D. a few

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：A（little）** —— Although 转折：有钱但花得"少"表否定；money 不可数用 little。

:::

**—Which jacket do you prefer, the black one or the brown one?
—I like ____. They are both too expensive.**

- A. both
- B. either
- C. neither
- D. none

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：C（neither）** —— 两件都太贵 → 两件都不喜欢，neither。

:::

**I don’t like this shirt. Can you show me ____?**

- A. other
- B. another
- C. the other
- D. others

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（another）** —— 不喜欢这件，再随便看一件（泛指三者以上中另一个），another。

:::

**—How many students passed the exam?
—____ of them. Everyone failed.**

- A. None
- B. No one
- C. Nothing
- D. Neither

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：A（None）** —— How many 提问 + of them 用 None；No one 不接 of。

:::

**I have two brothers. ____ is a teacher, and the other is a doctor.**

- A. Each
- B. Either
- C. One
- D. Another

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：C（One）** —— 两者：One is..., and the other is...。

:::

**The weather in Beijing is colder than ____ in Guangzhou in winter.**

- A. it
- B. that
- C. one
- D. this

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（that）** —— 比较对象 weather 单数、同类不同物，用 that。

:::
