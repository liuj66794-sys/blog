## 第一部分 · 考情分析与答题原则

| 命题特征 | 测试角度（考什么） | 答题角度（从哪下手） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. 文章短，挖空多 2. 情景意义选择为主，语法选择为辅 3. 短文逻辑性强，结构严谨，层次分明 4. 单词填空为主，短语词组整体填空为辅 5. 逻辑性强，实词为主，虚词为辅 6. 保留提示句 | 1. 词义辨析 2. 固定搭配 3. 语法结构 4. 文章的衔接及推理 | 1. 语法角度 2. 词语用法角度 3. 上下文角度 4. 惯用法角度 5. 常识和知识角度 |

::: info

三大答题原则
1. **完形服从完意**：根据整篇文章的意思决定哪个词最合适，而不是仅看单句语法或单词用法；填入的词必须在语境中有意义。

2. **单句服从段落**：一个句子内部多个选项看似合适时，要考虑该句在段落中的作用和位置，与段落主题保持一致。

3. **段落服从全文**：填入的词应推动文章发展，与全文中心思想契合。

:::

## 第二部分 · 解题三步

1. **跳过空格通读全文，把握大意**：第一遍通读获得整体印象，弄清文脉、抓住主旨。尤其要读懂**首句和末句**——首句帮助判断体裁、预测主要内容；末句帮助了解结局或作者态度。
2. **结合选项，综合考虑，初选答案**：联系上下文，从语法结构、词语搭配和选项中寻找提示；以词义、句义为先，再分析句子结构，综合比较、筛选并排除干扰项。
3. **先易后难，瞻前顾后，各个击破**：有把握的先做，没把握的先跳过；结合已确定的选项再读全文，随着理解深入，难度自然降低，正确率提高。

::: info

口诀**先完意，后完形；先全文，后单句。有把握先填，没把握先跳；全部回填，再读一遍。**

:::

## 第三部分 · 七大解题策略

### 策略一 · 语境推理

遇到难以直接选择的空，分析上下文，理解句间关系、段落结构与全文逻辑，把握文章走势，选择与作者思路或意图一致的选项。

- 上文有因，下文往往有果。
- 从对立两面阐述，中间常有转折词。
- 从同一角度论述，常出现并列连词。
- and 连接修饰人的形容词时，通常同为褒义或同为贬义；but 连接时，往往一褒一贬。

::: info

口诀**and 多同向，but 常反向；上因下果，前转后折。**

:::

### 策略二 · 运用语法理顺关系

- 注意**时态、人称、单复数**及数的变化，这类题往往容易得分。
- 主语从句：重点判断引导词与主谓一致。
- 宾语从句：重点判断引导词、语序和时态呼应（客观真理除外）。
- 定语从句：关键是关系词选择。
- 形容词、副词：结合语境判断原级、比较级或最高级，并注意各自用法。

### 策略三 · 复现解题法

复现包括**同义词、近义词和反义词**的原形或异形复现，主要考查整体篇章意识与上下文推断能力。课件三例连做——原词复现、同义词复现、反义词复现（课件未附答案，答案与解析为笔记整理补充）。

**【原词复现】In particular, there was (and perhaps still is) a belief in fairies. Not all of these ____ are the friendly, people-loving characters that appear in Disney films.**

- A. babies
- B. believes
- C. fairies
- D. supermen

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：C（fairies）** —— 上文已经出现 fairies，属于原词复现。

:::

**【同义词复现】Garth’s email was short, but I learned more from that ____ message than I ever did from a textbook.**

- A. accurate
- B. urgent
- C. brief
- D. humorous

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：C（brief）** —— brief（简短的）与前文的 short 同义复现。

:::

**【反义词复现】Life is full of challenges. Sometimes we face difficulties, but we should never give up. … Giving up is easy, but ____ requires courage.**

- A. stopping
- B. quitting
- C. persevering
- D. retreating

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：C（persevering）** —— persevering（坚持）与前面的 Giving up（放弃）构成反义复现。

:::

### 策略四 · 固定搭配解题法

这类题与语法结构题相似，但更强调**词与词的惯用搭配**，涉及关联词、动词、副词、形容词、名词和短语。先读下面这篇小短文，再做完三空（课件未附答案，答案与解析为笔记整理补充）。

I have a dream of becoming a successful writer. Writing has been my passion since I was a child. I remember that I used to **___1___** stories in my notebooks and show them to my parents. They always praised me for my creativity.

However, when I grew up, I realized that becoming a writer was not an easy task. I had to face many rejections from publishers. But I didn’t give **___2___**. I kept on sending my manuscripts to different publishing houses.

One day, I received a call from a publisher. He said that he was interested in my book and wanted to publish it. I was over the moon. I had finally achieved my dream **___3___** hard work and perseverance.

**I remember that I used to ____ stories in my notebooks and show them to my parents.**

- A. write up
- B. make up
- C. turn up
- D. pick up

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（make up）** —— make up stories 表示「编故事」。

:::

**But I didn’t give ____. I kept on sending my manuscripts to different publishing houses.**

- A. in
- B. out
- C. up
- D. away

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：C（up）** —— give up 表示「放弃」，与 kept on sending 呼应。

:::

**I had finally achieved my dream ____ hard work and perseverance.**

- A. through
- B. with
- C. by
- D. of

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：A（through）** —— through hard work and perseverance 表示「通过努力和坚持」。

:::

### 策略五 · 逻辑语气解题法

先了解全文**人物、时间、地点**等信息，再分析句间和段间关系。常见逻辑语气包括**并列、递进、因果、转折和委婉**；选项多为连词、副词或具有连词作用的短语。先读短文，再做完三空（课件未附答案，答案与解析为笔记整理补充）。

There are two main types of motivation: intrinsic and extrinsic. Intrinsic motivation comes from within oneself. It’s the kind of motivation that drives a person to do something **___1___** they enjoy the process or find it personally fulfilling. For example, an artist might paint because they love the act of creating and expressing themselves through colors and shapes.

Extrinsic motivation, **___2___**, comes from external factors. This could be rewards like money, praise, or recognition. A salesperson, for instance, might work hard to achieve high sales figures **___3___** they want to earn a big bonus.

**Intrinsic motivation comes from within oneself. It’s the kind of motivation that drives a person to do something ____ they enjoy the process or find it personally fulfilling.**

- A. because
- B. although
- C. unless
- D. until

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：A（because）** —— 前果后因：驱使人做事的原因是享受过程、获得满足，用 because。

:::

**Extrinsic motivation, ____, comes from external factors.**

- A. in addition
- B. on the other hand
- C. as a result
- D. in conclusion

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（on the other hand）** —— 内在动机 vs 外在动机构成对照，用 on the other hand（另一方面）。

:::

**A salesperson, for instance, might work hard to achieve high sales figures ____ they want to earn a big bonus.**

- A. but
- B. and
- C. or
- D. because

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：D（because）** —— 前果后因：努力冲业绩的原因是想拿大额奖金，用 because。

:::

### 策略六 · 利用文化背景和生活常识

完形填空以独立语篇命题，会交织各类常识和文化背景知识。解题时，语言知识以及**英美国家文化背景和生活常识**都可能发挥作用。

### 策略七 · 对比结构与平行结构

**对比结构**把两种对立事物，或同一事物的两个不同方面，并列起来比较；**平行结构**由结构相同或相似、意义密切关联、语法一致的词或句子成串排列，形式整齐，内容联系紧密（课件未附答案，答案与解析为笔记整理补充）。

A pupil who can do his homework in a quiet and **____** room is in a much better position than a pupil who does his homework in a small, noisy room with the television on.

**A pupil who can do his homework in a quiet and ____ room is in a much better position than a pupil who does his homework in a small, noisy room with the television on.**

- A. furnished
- B. expensive
- C. comfortable
- D. suitable

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：C（comfortable）** —— comfortable 与 quiet 形成同向的平行描述，并与后面的 small, noisy 构成对比。

:::

## 易错清单

::: danger 易错

高频易错
1. **只看单句，不看全文**：语法正确不等于语篇正确；先确认人物、事件、情感方向和文章主旨

2. **忽略首尾句**：首句常提示体裁和主题，末句常揭示结局或作者态度

3. **近义词凭中文选**：要比较搭配、语义色彩和使用对象，如 learn from、pay respects to、make a U-turn

4. **逻辑词不回读**：and/but/so/because 必须同时满足前后语义关系

5. **固定搭配拆开判断**：看到动词或介词空，要把前后组成整体，如 give up、pass down、depend on、look for

6. **忽略复现**：原词、同义词和反义词可能跨句甚至跨段出现

7. **漏查数与时态**：答案放回原句后，检查主谓一致、单复数、时态、语态和非谓语形式

8. **被课件错页误导**：练习十一 p28、练习十七 p43–44 的选项错页，以本课说明为准，不把重复选项当作对应题选项

:::

### 练习一 · 山西木雕

A piece of wood can tell the stories of Shanxi—its history, culture, scenes and local life, Ye Yaoliang said at a recent exhibition in the provincial capital of Taiyuan. Ye is a famous woodcarving（木雕）**___1___**.

According to Ye, Shanxi’s woodcarving has a history of nearly 2,000 years. For thousands of years, the art has been a way to show the local culture, with its **___2___** covering historical legends（传说）and landscapes（乡村风景画）. Humans, mountains, rivers, flowers, birds and animals are commonly **___3___** in the artworks.

::: details 本篇中文译文

Jim Grant 上班途中发现一栋楼冒出黑烟，便停车拨打 911，随后掉头绕回来再看一眼。他把车停到楼前，看到火焰从二楼窗户窜出。既没看见也没听见消防车赶来，Grant 便冲向侧门，跑上楼梯。

到了二楼，他踢响每一扇公寓门，大喊“快出去！”无人回应，他以为人们已经逃走。可是到走廊尽头时，他注意到一扇半开的门，便一脚踢开。屋里有一名坐轮椅、惊恐万分的女子，还有一个小男孩和一个小婴儿。Grant 大喊“快出去！”女子困惑地看着他，还说要换衣服。Grant 没有等待，他把婴儿紧抱在胸前，同时拖着男孩冲下走廊。

Grant 安全到达室外时，现场唯一能提供帮助的人是一名警察。他告诉警察那名女子仍在楼里，两人随即冲进烟雾弥漫的大楼。多亏他们，一家人获救；Grant 和警察也因勇气受到表彰。

:::
