## 第一部分 · 阅读理解 （第 1—15 题 · 建议 40 分钟）

### Passage A（第 1—5 题）

（首段）Since ancient times, frugality has been one of the traditional virtues among Chinese people. Following this traditional way of life, more young people in China are now looking to frugal, low-cost lifestyles in order to spend less and save more.

（第二段）They are beginning to realize and reject the pitfalls of consumerism, believing that simplicity can bring happiness and satisfaction.

（第三段）Economic and employment pressures, as well as changing attitudes toward material life, are also resulting in more young people turning their backs on extravagance and waste.

（第四段）Frugality is being practised in a number of ways. Some young people choose discount and low-priced goods, while others are replacing well-known brands with those that are cost-effective. Some are cautious when buying non-essential items, while others say they save all they can in order to make important purchases.

（课件未展示中间段落，从略。）

（末段）The student added, “As luxury food and brand-name clothing cannot increase my happiness, why not satisfy myself by spending less for the same amount of happiness?”

A. Plain needy lifestyles.

C. Simple low-cost lifestyles.

A. More young people in China.
B. More young people in every country.
C. More young people in any other countries.
D. More young people in many other countries.
3. Which is not the reason for more young people expecting a frugal life?
A. Rich and wealthy life.
B. Economic pressure.
C. Employment pressures.
D. Changing attitudes toward material life.
4. How many ways of frugality are there being practised in the passage?
A. 2.
B. 3.
C. 4.

A. More young people are in pursuit of happiness and satisfaction.

Everybody gets stressed time to time. __16__ Some ways of dealing with stress—like screaming or hitting someone—don’t solve much. But other ways, like talking to someone you trust, can lead you to solving your problem or at least feeling better.

Try taking these four steps the next time you are stressed:

Get support. When you need help, reach out to the people who care about you. Talk to a trusted adult, such as a parent or other relatives. __17__ They might have had similar problems, such as dealing with a test, or the death of a beloved pet.

Don’t take it out on yourself. Sometimes when kids are stressed and upset they take it out on themselves. Oh, dear, that’s no good idea. Remember that there are always people to help you. Don’t take it out on yourself. __18__

Try to solve the problem. After you’re calm and you have support from adults and friends, it’s time to get down to business. __19__ Even if you can’t solve it all, you can solve a piece of it.

Be positive. Most stress is temporary. Remember stress does go away, especially when you figure out the problem and start working on solving it. These steps aren’t magic, but they do work. And if you can stay positive as you make your way through a tough time, you’ll help yourself feel better even faster. __20__

B. Different people feel stress in different ways.
C. Ah, it feels so good when the stress is gone.

**36. 答案 tenth**　复合形容词所需序数词为 tenth。考点：序数词构成复合形容词（回炉 0005 课 · 数词和连词）

**37. 答案 hoping**　主句动作伴随希望看见星星，用现在分词 hoping。考点：现在分词作状语（回炉 0011 课 · 非谓语）

**38. 答案 of**　be afraid of 为固定搭配。

**39. 答案 softly**　修饰动词 sings 应用副词 softly；图片题面明确给出 (soft)。考点：副词修饰动词（回炉 0006 课 · 形容词和副词）

**40. 答案 warmth**　形容词性物主代词 her 后接名词 warmth。

**41. 答案 kisses**　一般现在时主语 She，谓语用 kisses。考点：一般现在时第三人称单数（回炉 0009 课 · 时态语态 / 0010 课 · 主谓一致）

**42. 答案 as**　as usual 为固定短语。

**43. 答案 told**　is told 构成一般现在时被动语态。考点：一般现在时被动语态（回炉 0009 课 · 时态语态）

**44. 答案 for**　be late for school 为固定搭配。

**45. 答案 Thousands**　thousands of 表示“成千上万”，句首首字母大写。考点：thousands of 不确切数量（回炉 0005 课 · 数词和连词）
