题型技巧 · 第六课

10 空 × 1.5 分 = 15 分——有提示，看词性；无提示，看成分；答案放回全文复读。

::: info 考点地图

1. **题型**：10 空 × 1.5 分 = 15 分（教材上册 P240-254）；以语篇为载体，设空分**无提示词型**与**给提示词型**
2. **无提示词五技巧**：缺主宾填代词 → 名词前限定词 → 介词 → 特殊句式 → 句间连词
3. **有提示词四技巧**：名词（单复数 / 所有格 / 转形容词）、形容词（转副词 / 否定词缀 / 比较级最高级）、动词（谓语 vs 非谓语）、词性转换总表
4. **实战**：2025 / 2024 真题 + 题海 15 篇（原题转四选一课堂练习）

:::

## 考情分析（教材 P240；技巧课 p3-4）

命题特点：文章一般设 **10 处空白**，分为**无提示词**与**给提示词**两类。核心考点包括**非谓语、构词法、时态、语态、主谓一致和三大从句**。

| 设空类型 | 重点考查 |
| --- | --- |
| **有提示词** | 名词、动词、代词、形容词/副词、数词、词性转换 |
| **无提示词** | 冠词、并列连词或从属连词、介词、代词 |

## 解题技巧 · 无提示词（技巧课 p5-14）

### 技巧 1 · 句子缺主语或宾语，通常填代词或名词

I can send a message to Kenya whenever I want to, and **____** gets there almost in a second.
我可以随时给肯尼亚发信息，而且信息几乎一秒就能到达那里。

::: info

答案解析**it** —— and 连接两个分句，后一分句**缺主语**；所指对象是前文的 the message，用代词 it 替代。

:::

### 技巧 2 · 名词前缺限定词，优先考虑限定词

若名词前没有**冠词、形容词性物主代词、形容词或不定代词**等限定成分，设空处很可能需要限定词。

It is said that a short-tempered man in the Song Dynasty was very anxious to help **____** rice crop grow up quickly.
据说宋朝有个急性子的人，非常着急地想让他的禾苗快快长高。

::: info

答案解析**his** —— rice crop 前缺限定词；故事中的禾苗属于这个人，应填形容词性物主代词 his。

:::

### 技巧 3 · 句子成分完整，而名词或代词前有空，考虑介词

- 当句子不缺主语、宾语或表语时，名词/代词前的空通常填**介词**。
- 介词后接**名词、代词或动名词**。

...who should have the honour of receiving me **____** a guest in their house.
……谁有幸把我当作客人接待到家中。

::: info

答案解析**as** —— a guest 不作主语、表语或动词宾语，需要介词引出；as 表示“当作”。

:::

### 技巧 4 · 用特殊句式判断

| 特殊句式 | 课件例句 | 答案与依据 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **强调句** It is/was...that... | ...and ____ was only after I heard she became sick that I learned she couldn't eat MSG（味精）! | **it** —— 句子是 It was...that... 强调结构。 |
| **it 作形式主语或形式宾语** | ...as ____ took them just three minutes to steal paintings by two world-famous artists... | **it** —— 句型为 It takes sb. some time to do sth.，不定式 to steal paintings 是真正主语。 |
| **so/such...that...** | This made the goat so jealous ____ it began plotting against（谋划对付）the donkey. | **that** —— 固定结构为 so...that...。 |

### 技巧 5 · 两个成分或两个句子之间缺连接，考虑连词

- 两个或多个单词/短语之间没有连词，可能填 **and, or** 等并列连词；填并列连词时判断**顺承、转折**等逻辑。
- 两个句子之间**既无连词，又无分号或句号**，一定要填并列连词或从属连词；填从属连词时先划出从句，再判断从句类型。

| 从句类型 | 引导词判断 |
| --- | --- |
| **名词性从句**（主、宾、表、同位语） | 不作从句成分：that, whether、“四何”；作从句成分：who, whom, what, which, whose |
| **定语从句** | 关系代词：that, which, who, whom, whose, as；关系副词：when, where, why |
| **状语从句** | 先判断具体状语关系，再选相应引导词 |

...two world-famous artists, Pablo Picasso **____** Candido Portinari, which are worth millions of dollars.
……两位世界著名艺术家巴勃罗·毕加索和坎迪多·波尔蒂纳里的画作，价值数百万美元。　**答案：and** —— 两个人名并列，需要并列连词。

Please send us postcards **and** we'll know where you have visited.
请给我们寄明信片，这样我们就会知道你去过哪里。

We were having a heated discussion **when** there was a power failure.
我们正在激烈讨论，这时突然停电了。（课件原文 a failure power 按语义修正为 a power failure。）

## 解题技巧 · 有提示词（技巧课 p15-25）

### 技巧 6 · 提示词为名词

**① 单复数**：前有数词或 these, those, several, many, all, both, various, numerous 等词时用复数；前有 one of 时用复数；谓语动词为复数时主语空格也可能需要名词复数。

Air pollution is caused by the following three **____**（reason）: about half of the problem is caused by vehicles.
空气污染由以下三个原因造成：其中约一半的问题由车辆引起。

::: info

答案解析**reasons** —— three 后接可数名词复数。

:::

**② 所有格**：单数名词和不以 -s 结尾的复数名词，词尾通常加 's；以 -s/-es 结尾的复数名词，形式为 -s'/-es'。

There are many students living at school; the **____**（child）houses are all far from school.
许多学生住在学校，因为孩子们的家都离学校很远。

::: info

答案解析**children's** —— 不规则复数 children 不以 s 结尾，所有格加 's。

:::

**③ 名词转形容词**：常见位置有 a/an/the + 形容词 + 名词、副词后、系动词后、make/let/have + sb. + 形容词。

He immediately felt **____**（silence）as tears flew down from his big blue eyes.
泪水从他蓝色的大眼睛里涌出时，他立刻沉默下来。

::: info

答案解析**silent** —— felt 是系动词，后接形容词作表语。

:::

### 技巧 7 · 提示词为形容词

**① 形容词转副词**：副词可修饰形容词、动词或全句；常见位置包括“副词 + 形容词”“主语 + 副词 + 谓语”“助动词 + 副词 + 实义动词”。构词规则：

1. 多数形容词直接加 -ly。
2. 辅音字母 + y：变 y 为 i 再加 -ly，如 happy → happily。
3. 辅音字母 + le：去 e 加 y，如 terrible → terribly, possible → possibly。
4. 以 ue 结尾：去 e 加 -ly，如 true → truly。
5. 以 ll 结尾：直接加 y，如 full → fully。
6. 以 c 结尾：词尾加 -ally，如 basic → basically。
7. 个别例外：whole → wholly。

Jack **____**（deep）loves Rose.　杰克深爱着露丝。　**答案：deeply** —— 副词修饰动词 loves。

She can **____**（quick）adapt to the new class.　她能很快适应新班级。　**答案：quickly** —— 副词修饰动词 adapt。

**② 添加否定词缀**：根据句意和逻辑，在词根前加 un-, im-, dis- 等，或在词根后加 -less：successful → unsuccessful; possible → impossible; honest → dishonest; help → helpless。

**③ 比较级与最高级**：

- much, far, still, even, rather, a little, a bit, a lot, a great deal, than 常提示**比较级**。
- 范围标志 in, of, among 常提示**最高级**；one of the、the + 序数词 常接最高级。
- 固定句型：the + 比较级..., the + 比较级...；比较级 + and + 比较级；“否定词 + 比较级”表达最高级含义。
- 即使没有 than，也要根据**隐含比较**判断。

| 课件例句 | 答案与依据 |
| --- | --- |
| If you work harder, your English will be ____（good）. | **better** —— 句意含前后比较。 |
| The elephant is ____（big）than the horse. | **bigger** —— than 提示比较级。 |
| Your sweater is much ____（expensive）than mine. | **more expensive** —— much...than 修饰比较级。 |

### 技巧 8 · 提示词为动词：先判断谓语还是非谓语

**① 谓语动词**：句中没有其他谓语，或设空动词与已有谓语构成并列时，考虑**时态、语态和主谓一致**。

| 课件例句 | 答案 | 判断依据 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Each child ____（offer）an apple at the party yesterday. | **was offered** | 一般过去时的被动语态。 |
| Look! The students ____（clean）the classroom. | **are cleaning** | Look! 提示现在进行时。 |
| I ____（live）in this city for 5 years. | **have lived** | for 5 years 提示现在完成时。 |
| By the time he arrived at the station, the train ____（leave）. | **had left** | 火车离开发生在过去动作 arrived 之前。 |
| The girl, with her parents, ____（wait）at the school gate now. | **is waiting** | with 不改变主语，谓语与 the girl 一致。 |

**② 非谓语动词**：句中已有谓语且设空处不与之并列时，用非谓语：

- **作主语或宾语**：通常用 -ing 表示一般情况，用不定式表示具体或将来的情况。**Going to bed on time** is a good habit.（按时睡觉是一个好习惯。）
- **作目的状语，或在形容词后作状语**：通常用不定式。**To complete the project as planned**, we'll have to work two more hours a day.（为按计划完成项目，我们每天还得多工作两小时。）
- **作定语**：现在分词表示主动、正在进行；being done 表示被动且正在发生；过去分词表示被动、完成；不定式表示目的或将来。

### 技巧 9 · 词性转换总表

| 句子位置 / 功能 | 用什么词性 |
| --- | --- |
| 作表语、定语或补语 | 常用**形容词** |
| 作主语，或在及物动词/介词后作宾语 | 用**名词** |
| 形容词性物主代词后，或“冠词（+ 形容词）”后 | 用**名词** |
| 修饰动词、形容词、副词或全句，作状语 | 用**副词** |
| 根据句意和前后逻辑构成反义词 | 词根前加 un-, im- 等，或词根后加 -less 等 |

## 易错清单

::: danger 易错

高频易错
1. 有提示词不能机械变形：先判断句子缺什么成分，再决定词性、时态、语态和数

2. 动词题先找全句谓语：已有谓语且无并列连词时，优先考虑非谓语

3. 无提示词先查句子是否缺主语/宾语，再查名词前限定词、介词搭配和句间连词

4. **which** 可引导非限制性定语从句并指代前面整件事；**that** 不能用于逗号后的非限制性定语从句

5. 系动词后用形容词；修饰动词、形容词或全句用副词

6. 比较级不只看 than，也要看 much/even/still、固定句型和隐含比较

7. 答案必须放回全文复读，检查语法、逻辑、大小写和拼写

:::

### 连词随堂练习（技巧课 p14）

**【连词随堂 · 原题为填空】This is the best film ____ I have ever seen. 这是我看过的最好的电影。**

- A. which
- B. what
- C. that
- D. who

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：C（that）** —— 课件原答案：先行词由最高级 the best 修饰，限制性定语从句用 that。

:::

**【连词随堂 · 原题为填空】My friend showed me around the town, ____ was very kind of him. 我的朋友带我参观了这座城镇，他这样做真是太好了。**

- A. that
- B. which
- C. what
- D. it

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（which）** —— 课件原答案：非限制性定语从句用 which 指代前面整件事；that 不能用于逗号后的非限制性定语从句。

:::

**【连词随堂 · 原题为填空】____ he wants to go there is obvious. 很明显，他想去那里。**

- A. That
- B. What
- C. Which
- D. As

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：A（That）** —— 课件原答案：that 引导主语从句，在从句中不作成分。

:::

**【连词随堂 · 原题为填空】____ he knocked at the door, I was working at the table. 当他敲门时，我正在桌旁工作。**

- A. Because
- B. Which
- C. Unless
- D. When

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：D（When）** —— 课件原答案：表示「当……时」，when 引导时间状语从句。

:::

### 真题一 · 2025 年（技巧课 p26-29）

Last weekend, as snow fell heavily in North Andover, Firefighter Tim of the North Andover Fire Department responded to **36 ______** call about an accident. Tim **37 ______**（hurry）to the scene and discovered a mother and her son inside the vehicle. **38 ______**（fortunate）, the boy and his mother were unharmed. But as the tow truck arrived to take away their damaged car, the child stood in the cold, watching the scene unfold.

“He was actually **39 ______**（wear）a Marvel coat that night, so I talked to him **40 ______** some superheroes he liked,” Tim said.

The real superhero of the night was Tim, **41 ______** draped his turnout coat over the little boy as he watched his mother's car get towed away. This simple but **42 ______**（power）action that Tim did offered the little boy warmth and comfort in the storm. A photo capturing the moment, **43 ______**（share）by the fire department on Facebook, quickly gained attention, with commenters praising the firefighter's compassion.

“All the **44 ______**（reaction）online are incredible. All the positive comments people have left just makes me feel really proud of **45 ______**（I）and the job I get to do every day,” Tim said.

**【2025 真题 · 无提示词】...Firefighter Tim of the North Andover Fire Department responded to ____ call about an accident.**

- A. an
- B. the
- C. one
- D. a

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：D（a）** —— 原题为无提示词填空（课件原答案）：call 是可数名词单数，此处泛指「一通事故电话」，用不定冠词 a。

:::

- B. hurries
- C. hurrying
- D. to hurry

**答案：A（hurried）** —— 原题为有提示词填空：全文叙述过去事件，且后文用 discovered，用一般过去时。

- A. Fortunate
- B. Unfortunately
- C. Fortune
- D. Fortunately

**答案：D（Fortunately）** —— 原题为有提示词填空：副词作评注性状语修饰全句，位于句首首字母大写。

:::

- A. wearing
- B. wore
- C. worn
- D. wears

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：A（wearing）** —— 原题为有提示词填空：与 was 构成过去进行时，表示当时正穿着漫威外套。

:::

**【2025 真题 · 无提示词】“...so I talked to him ____ some superheroes he liked,” Tim said.**

- A. with
- B. about
- C. on
- D. at

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（about）** —— 原题为无提示词填空：固定搭配 talk to sb. about sth.。

:::

**【2025 真题 · 无提示词】The real superhero of the night was Tim, ____ draped his turnout coat over the little boy as he watched his mother’s car get towed away.**

- A. which
- B. who
- C. whom
- D. whose

- C. shares

::: details 点开核对答案

- C. myself
- D. my

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：C（myself）** —— 原题为有提示词填空：反身代词作 of 的宾语，并与主语 I 呼应。

### 真题二 · 2024 年（技巧课 p30-32）

On his tenth birthday, Tim woke to the news of a serious earthquake in the city where he was born. Living in another country but **36 ______**（name）after his birth city, Tim felt he was **37 ______**（deep）connected to the disaster. Driven by a strong desire to help, he **38 ______**（see）an opportunity to make a difference through his love of running. Two years before, Tim had run his first half-marathon, raising money for **39 ______** annual charity event for kids.

This time he set a new goal, **40 ______** was to run ten kilometers ten times, each run marking a year of his life. He was determined **41 ______**（use）this challenge to raise funds for the earthquake relief. Over the course of four months, he ran a total of 100 kilometers — more than two marathons. In his final run, the local charity center succeeded **42 ______** organizing a community event. Tim was joined by his father, his teacher and **43 ______**（member）of the local community.

In the end, Tim's project raised more than $100,000 for the earthquake victims. “Sometimes I felt **44 ______**（tire）. I remind **45 ______**（I）of local people. I'm helping them and I'm happy.”

**【2024 真题 · 提示词 name】Living in another country but ____ after his birth city, Tim felt he was deeply connected to the disaster.**

- A. named
- B. naming
- C. to name

### 练习十四 · 校园节约行动（题海 P13）

How's it going? Are you busy with your study these days?

I've been back at school **1. ______** nearly four weeks. I'm very glad to tell you **2. ______** great changes have taken place in our school this term. First of all, we're **3. ______**（ask）to “clear our plates” when having our meals and say no to wasting. Some of us **4. ______**（use）to order more than what we could eat. That was a big waste of food. Now we need to finish the food we order. We should also stop **5. ______**（waste）in some other ways. For example, we should turn off the lights **6. ______** we leave the classroom. And our school has opened up some fields for us to learn how **7. ______** grow vegetables.

Each class is given a small garden and our class has decided to grow some **8. ______**（tomato）and beans in our free time. I think that'll be very interesting. Maybe I'll be able to post you some beans we've grown by **9. ______**（we）next time! What's more, we have only two classes in the afternoon so we have **10. ______**（much）time for after-school activities than before. I'm one of the traffic safety volunteers in our school. After school, we take turns to go to the streets near our school and ask people to follow the traffic rules.

I think we are having a different school life now!

Please write back soon and tell me more about your school.

**【练习十四 · 无提示词】I’ve been back at school ____ nearly four weeks.**

- B. for
- C. in
- D. at

**答案：B（for）** —— 笔记补充解析（原题为无提示词填空）：for + 时间段与现在完成时连用。

### 练习十五 · 旅途中的思乡（题海 P13）

It was an amazing week for my husband and me — the trip of a lifetime. Months ago, when my husband told me that he **2. ______**（will）have a meeting in London, I expressed my wish to go to Europe together with him. Then we asked his mother to look after our two **3. ______**（child）and started off.

During the week's time, we visited London and Paris — as much as we **4. ______**（can）: Big Ben, the Louvre and so on. All these were beautiful places we expected to see before. We really enjoyed ourselves.

On our last night in Paris, after we enjoyed the night view of the Eiffel Tower, my husband called home. His mother answered the phone. In a second, my husband's face fell and he looked so **5. ______**（worry）. I could feel something bad happened.

He didn't answer and continued to listen. A few minutes later, he said sadly to me that Tony, our seven-year-old son, **6. ______**（have）fallen off his bike and broken his leg. He must be sent to hospital at once.

At that moment, Paris **7. ______**（sudden）lost its charm.

We hurried back to our hotel and then to the airport...

Finally, we got home. We rushed into our children's bedroom. **8. ______**（see）our two children, I suddenly realized the **9. ______**（true）: there is no **10. ______**（sweet）sight in the world than your children's faces that greet you at home.
