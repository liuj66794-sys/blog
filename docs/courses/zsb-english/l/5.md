## 第一部分 · 数词

数词（num.）是表示**数目多少或顺序**的词。

### 1. 基数词：确切 vs 不确切数量

::: info

口诀**0–12 特殊记；13–19 加 teen（13、15、18 特殊记）；整十加 ty（20、30、40、50、80 特殊记）**

:::

| 类型 | 规则 | 例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **确切数量** | hundred/thousand/million/billion 前有具体数词 → 用**单数**、**不接 of** | two hundred；three thousand；11 million（一千一百万）；a quarter of billion（十亿的四分之一） |
| **不确切数量** | 泛指 → 用**复数 + of**，前不能有具体数词（可被 many/several/a few 修饰） | hundreds of people；several thousands of 数千；tens of millions of |

There are **hundreds of** people in the park.（好几百人）
There are **three hundred** people in the park.（三百人）

### 2. 序数词

::: info

口诀
**123 特殊记，其余都加 th；8 去 t，9 去 e；ve 要用 f 替；见 y 变 ie，词尾再加 th**

first / second / third · eighth · ninth · fifth / twelfth · twentieth

:::

1. 序数词前一般加 **the**：**The first** chair is Li Ping's.
2. 表"又一、再一"可用 a/an（first 除外）：Try **a second** time and you'll succeed.
3. 前有物主代词、名词所有格时**不再用 the**：**My first class** ✔
4. 与基数词连用时**序数词在前**：**the first three** pages
5. 修饰谓语动词时 the 省掉：The team ranks **first** in its country.

### 3. 分数、小数、百分数

**分数**：分子基数词、分母序数词；**分子大于 1，分母加 s**（8/9 → eight ninths；4/5 → four fifths）。分母是 2/4 用 half / quarter。也可用 in / out of，分子分母都用基数词、分子在前：八分之五 = **five out of eight** = **five in eight**。

**小数**：点读 point，零读 zero/o，小数点后逐位读：53.16 → fifty-three point one six

**百分数**：基数词 + percent：100 percent。**分数/百分数 + of + 名词作主语时，谓语与 of 后的名词一致**：

**Two thirds of** the money **was** spent on food.

### 4. 倍数的四种句型

一倍 once，两倍 twice/double，三倍以上"基数词 + times"。

| 句型 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| 倍数 + as + 原级 + as | This room is **twice as big as** that one. |
| 倍数 + 比较级 + than | This rope is **four times longer than** that one. |
| 倍数 + the + size/length/width + of | This avenue is **twice the width of** that one. |
| 比较级 + by + 倍数 | This rope is longer than that one **by four times**. |

::: info

口诀**as 原级 as 连，than 前必有比较级，有 by 倍数在其后，the 后名词 of 现**

:::

## 第二部分 · 连词

并列连词连接**对等成分**（词+词、短语+短语、句+句）；从属连词引导三大从句。

::: info

对等成分
词 + 词：Would you like tea **or** coffee?

短语 + 短语：We will go to the park **after lunch or in the morning**.

:::

**从属连词总览**：

- 引导**名词性从句**——不做成分：that、whether/if、when/where/why/how；做成分：who/whom/what/which/whose
- 引导**定语从句**——关系代词：that, which, who/whom/whose, as；关系副词：when, where, why
- 引导**状语从句**：when, while, as, where, because, if, although, even if 等

（详见定语从句、名词性从句、状语从句三课。）

| 关系 | 连词 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 并列 | and | I like playing basketball, football **and** volleyball. |
| 并列 | both...and... | **Both** Jim **and** Mike **are** going to the Great Wall.（谓语复数） |
| 并列 | neither...nor... | **Neither** the teacher **nor the students are** in the classroom.（就近） |
| 并列 | not only...but (also)... | **Not only** Lily **but also** her parents **are** friendly.（就近） |
| 转折 | but | I love you **but** I don't believe you. |
| 转折 | yet | It's a small car, **yet** it's very spacious. |
| 转折 | while（对比） | I love tea **while** my father loves coffee. |
| 选择 | or（否则 or else / otherwise） | Do you go to work by bike **or** by bus? / Put the gun down, **or else**! |
| 选择 | either...or... | **Either** he could not come **or** he did not want to. |
| 选择 | not...but... | She is **not** a teacher **but** a student. |
| 因果 | so（表结果） | It was late, **so** I went home. |
| 因果 | for（推断性原因，分句不能放句首） | It must have rained last night, **for** it is wet all over. |

::: danger 易错

不能连用**but 和 although 不能在同一句连用**（so 和 because 同理）：

✔ Although I am not good-looking, I am very gentle.

✔ I am not good-looking, **but** I am very gentle.

✘ Although I am not good-looking, but...

:::

::: info

but/yet vs however
**but/yet 是连词**，直接连两句；**however/nevertheless 是副词**，需句号/分号隔开再接逗号：He has a good job. **However,** he never has money.

:::

## 易错清单

::: danger 易错

高频易错
1. two hundred ✔ / two hundreds ✘；hundreds of 前不能加具体数词

2. nine → **ninth**（去 e 加 th）；eight → eighth（去 t）

3. 序数词前有物主代词/名词所有格就不加 the

4. 分子大于 1 分母加 s：two thirds；of 后名词决定主谓一致

5. 倍数四句型要背熟

6. but 与 although、so 与 because 不能连用

7. **就近原则三兄弟**：neither...nor / not only...but also / either...or；both...and 谓语用复数

8. however/nevertheless 是副词

9. for 表推断性原因，分句不能放句首

:::

## 课堂练习

**—How many people will come to Beijing for the Olympic Games?
—It’s hard to say. ____ people, I think.**

- A. Million of
- B. Millions of
- C. Two millions of
- D. Two million of

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（Millions of）** —— 不确切数量：复数 + of，前面不能有具体数词。

:::

**September is the ____ month of a year.**

- A. nineth
- B. ninth
- C. ten
- D. tenth

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（ninth）** —— nine 变序数词去 e 加 th：ninth（不是 nineth）。

:::

**It was my grandfather’s ____ birthday. We enjoyed ourselves.**

- A. seventy
- B. seventieth
- C. the seventieth
- D. seventeenth

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（seventieth）** —— "第 70 个生日"用序数词 seventieth；前有名词所有格 grandfather’s，不再加 the。

:::

**She didn’t study hard, ______ she failed the test.**

- A. and
- B. but
- C. or
- D. so

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：D（so）** —— 前因后果：没努力学习，所以挂科了。

:::

**You can either stay at home ______ go out with us.**

- A. and
- B. or
- C. but
- D. nor

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（or）** —— either...or... 固定搭配。

:::

**Neither he ______ his brother likes swimming.**

- A. and
- B. or
- C. nor
- D. but

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：C（nor）** —— neither...nor... 固定搭配。

:::

**She was tired, ______ she kept working.**

- A. and
- B. but
- C. or
- D. so

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（but）** —— 前后转折：累但是继续工作。

:::

**Both Tom ______ Lily like playing basketball.**

- A. and
- B. or
- C. but
- D. nor

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：A（and）** —— both...and... 固定搭配，谓语用复数。

:::
