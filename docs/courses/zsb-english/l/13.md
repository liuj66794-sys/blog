## 第一部分 · 什么是名词性从句（教材 P83/P115）

名词表示人或事物的名称，在句中作**主语、宾语、表语、同位语**等。本该由**名词**充当这些位置、现在由**一个句子**来充当——这个句子就是**名词性从句**。

四种从句的由来（同一句话放进不同位置）：

| 例句 | 从句类型 |
| --- | --- |
| **Ma Yun is rich** is true.（原本：The fact is true.） | **主语从句** |
| I know **that Ma Yun is rich**.（原本：I know the fact.） | **宾语从句** |
| The fact is **that Ma Yun is rich**.（原本：The fact.） | **表语从句** |
| The fact **that Ma is rich** is true.（说明 the fact 的内容） | **同位语从句** |

## 第二部分 · 名词性从句的引导词（教材 P116）

### 1. 不作从句成分的三类引导词（从句不缺成分时用）

- **① that**：不作成分；无含义：**That** he misspelled the word made everyone happy. 他拼错了这个单词，这让大家都笑了。
- **② whether**：不作成分；含义"是否"：I wonder **whether** I should tell her the truth. 我不知道是否应该告诉她真相。They also surveyed **whether** they had a family dog or not.**Whether** I should tell her the truth is unknown.
- **③ 连接副词"四条大何" when / where / why / how**：不作主要成分，**有含义**：He doesn't know **why** he can find a girlfriend.

::: info

小结当名词性从句中**不缺成分**时，可以用 **that / whether / 四条大何（when/where/why/how）**来引导。

:::

### 2. 作从句成分的连接代词"5 个 W"（从句缺成分时用）

| 引导词 | 所作成分 | 含义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| who | 主语 | 谁 |
| whom | 宾语 | 谁 |
| what | 主、宾 | 什么（物） |
| which | 定语 | 哪一个 |
| whose | 定语 | 谁的 |

**What** she said left a deep impression on me.（what 作 said 的宾语）
Do you know **who** won the game?（who 作从句主语）
I know the girl **whose** hair is long and straight.（whose 作定语）

### 3. 四步解题法

::: info

口诀**划从句 → 看类型 → 看成分 → 看含义**

① **划从句**：从引导词（或设空处）开始划，划到引导词后（空后）的第二个谓语动词之前（若引导词后只有一个谓语动词，则划到句子最后）

② **看类型**：根据从句的位置判断从句类型

③ **看成分**：从句缺主语/宾语/定语 → 从"作成分"的 5W 中选；不缺成分 → 从"不作成分"三类中选

④ **看含义**：删掉引导词后从句含义完整 → 引导词无含义（that）

:::

### 4. 名词性从句的语序（教材 P116 补充）

::: danger 易错

一律陈述语序名词性从句常用**陈述语序（主语 + 谓语）**，不用疑问语序：

**That he won this game was** surprising. 他赢得了这场比赛，这很令人惊讶。
**When we can begin the project is** still under discussion. 我们何时才能开始这项工程仍在讨论中。

:::

## 第三部分 · 主语从句（教材 P117）

在复合句中充当**主语**成分的句子叫主语从句。

### 拓展 · 常用 it 作形式主语（4 种句型）

| 句型 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| It is + **名词** + 从句 | It is a fact **that** the earth goes round the sun. |
| It is + **形容词** + that | It is doubtful **that** safety is the real goal. |
| It is + **分词** + that | It is reported **that** she will go abroad soon. |
| It + **不及物动词**（happen, doesn't matter...）+ that | It happened **that** I saw her three days ago. |

It is/was said/reported/believed/estimated...that... = sb./sth. be + said/reported/believed/estimated to do（据说/据报道/据信/据估计……）。

## 第四部分 · 宾语从句（教材 P119–120）

置于**动词、介词**后起宾语作用的从句。三类：

- **动词**宾语从句：We believe **that he is honest**. 我们相信他是诚实的。
- **介词**宾语从句：I agree with **what you said just now**. 我同意你刚才说的话。
- **形容词**宾语从句：I am happy **that you can join us**. 你能加入我们，我很高兴。

### 1. that 引导的宾语从句

that 不作成分、无实际意义，只起连接作用，**口语和非正式文体中可省略**。但以下 5 种情况不能省略：

::: danger 易错

that 不能省略
1. **find/believe/think/consider/make + it + adj. + that 宾从**句型中不省略：I think **it** necessary **that** we take plenty of hot water every day.

2. 宾语从句是**双宾语中的直接宾语**时：I want to tell you **that** this problem is very common.

3. 宾语从句前有**插入语**时：We hope, on the contrary, **that** he will stay at home with us.

4. 两个或以上并列的 that 宾从：**第一个可省，其余不省**：He said (that) the book was very interesting and **that** all the children like to read it.

5. 宾语从句的主语是 **this/that** 或被 this/that 修饰时：The teacher said **that** **that** word was wrong.

:::

### 2. 用 whether 不用 if 的 7 种情况

**whether** 可以引导**所有**名词性从句；**if** 只能引导**宾语从句**表"是否"，多见于口语或非正式语境（常见于 ask、know、wonder、doubt 等动词后）：

1. 动词 **discuss, decide, doubt** 后：We discussed **whether** we should go there by plane.
2. **whether or not** 结构中：Let me know **whether** you can come or not.
3. 宾语从句**置于句首**时：**Whether** it is true or not, I can't tell. 这是不是真的，我无法判断。
4. **介词后**：They are talking about **whether** he will win the game.
5. whether 与 if 含义易混时：Please let me know **whether** you want to go.
6. **whether to do**：I can't decide **whether to stay**.
7. 引导**主语从句、表语从句、同位语从句**时：**Whether** the football game will be played depends on the weather.

## 第五部分 · 表语从句（教材 P121+123）

置于**系动词后**起表语作用的从句。引导词：除 if 外的一般引导词 + why + because + as though/if。

::: info

三组固定结构
1. **This/That/It is/was because...**：那是因为……He was late. **That was because** he was caught in a traffic jam. 那是因为他遇上了堵车。

2. **This/That/It is/was why...**：那就是……的原因He was caught in a traffic jam. **That was why** he was late. 那就是他迟到的原因。

3. 主语为 **reason** 时，表语从句连接词用 **that**：The reason was **that** he was caught in a traffic jam.

:::

## 第六部分 · 同位语从句（教材 P123）

作某一**抽象名词**的同位语，一般位于该名词之后，**解释和说明该名词的具体内容**。常见名词：**idea**（主意）、**fact**（事实）、**news**（新闻）、**hope**（希望）、**belief**（信念）、**thought**（想法）、**doubt**（怀疑）、**promise**（承诺）、**suggestion**（建议）、**order**（命令）、**possibility**（可能）等。

**引导词**：**that**（无实义，**不可省略**）；when、where、why、how、whether 等。

Some people have the idea **that you can cross Canada in less than five days**. 有人认为穿越加拿大只需要不到五天。
The student asked me the question **whether the book was worth reading**. 学生问我这本书是否值得阅读。

::: info

同位语从句 vs 定语从句
**定语从句**：that 是关系代词，**充当句子成分**，作宾语时可省略；**同位语从句**：that 是连词，**不充当成分**，一般不可省略。

The suggestion **that he raised at the meeting** is very good.（that 作 raised 的宾语 → **定语从句**）
The suggestion **that the students should have plenty of exercise** is very good.（that 不作成分，说明 suggestion 内容 → **同位语从句**）

:::

## 第七部分 · 宾语从句中的否定转移（导图专题补充）

当主句谓语是 **think / believe / suppose / expect** 等表观点的动词时，否定**从句**的 not 通常**转移到主句**：

I **don't think** he **is** right.（= I think he is not right. 我认为他不对。）
I **don't believe** she **will come**.（我认为她不会来。）

反意疑问句与之配合：I don't think he is right, **is he**?（陈述部分否定已前移，附加问句用肯定）

## 易错清单

::: danger 易错

高频易错
1. **if 与 whether 七区别**：介词后、句首、to do、or not 连用、discuss/decide/doubt 后、主/表/同位语从句——全用 whether

2. **名词性从句一律用陈述语序**（Could you tell me how you... ✘ how do you）

3. **reason 作主语，表语从句用 that**，不用 because（The reason is that...）

4. **That is because**（那是因为，后接原因）/ **That is why**（那就是为什么，后接结果）方向相反

5. **同位语从句 vs 定语从句**：that 作不作成分；同位语 that 不可省

6. **it 作形式主语/形式宾语**：think it necessary that...；It is reported that... = sb. is reported to do

7. **What...is that...** 主语从句缺主语用 What；**As 不能单独引导主语从句作主语**（需逗号非限或 It is known that）

8. **whoever = anyone who**（泛指）≠ who（特指）

:::

**—Do you know ____ the meeting will start?
—At 3:00 this afternoon.**

- A. when
- B. where
- C. why
- D. how

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：A（when）** —— 答语 At 3:00 是时间 → when。

:::

**____ is known to us all is that the 2022 Winter Olympics will be held in Beijing.**

- A. As
- B. What
- C. It
- D. That

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（What）** —— 主语从句缺主语 → What；As 不能引导带谓语 is 的主语从句放句首。

:::

**____ is reported that the 2022 Winter Olympics will be held in Beijing.**

- A. As
- B. What
- C. It
- D. That

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：C（It）** —— It is reported that... 固定句型（It 作形式主语）。

:::

**____ is known to us all that the 2022 Winter Olympics will be held in Beijing.**

- A. as
- B. what
- C. it
- D. that

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：C（it）** —— It is known to us all that... 固定句型。

:::

**Along with the letter was his promise ____ he would visit me this coming Christmas.**

- A. which
- B. that
- C. whether
- D. what

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（that）** —— promise 后同位语从句 → that。

:::

**Juliet has gone to Milan and I wonder ____ she can make it to（赶到）our wedding or not.**

- A. when
- B. that
- C. if
- D. whether

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：D（whether）** —— 与 or not 连用必须用 whether，不用 if。

:::

**We consider ____ he should clean the room by himself every day.**

- A. that it necessary
- B. necessary it that
- C. it that necessary
- D. it necessary that

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：D（it necessary that）** —— consider + it + necessary + that：it 作形式宾语，语序为 it necessary that。

:::

**In order to search for the escaped prisoner, the police decide to question ____ comes along on this road.**

- A. who
- B. whom
- C. whoever
- D. whomever

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：C（whoever）** —— 课件原解析：从句中 comes 为谓语，其前缺主语，排除 B/D；who 引导宾语从句通常表特指，whoever = anyone who 表泛指"无论谁"，符合"盘问所有出现在这条路上的人"。

:::

## 口诀卡

::: info

本课全部口诀
四步法：**划从句 → 看类型 → 看成分 → 看含义**

引导词：**不缺成分 → that（无义）/ whether（是否）/ 四条大何（when/where/why/how 有义）；缺成分 → 5W 连接代词**

表语从句：**reason → that；That is because 原因，That is why 结果**

whether 必用：**介前、句首、to do、or not、讨论决定怀疑（discuss/decide/doubt）、主表同位**

同位语从句抽象名词：**idea, fact, news, hope, belief, thought, doubt, promise, suggestion, order, possibility**

:::
