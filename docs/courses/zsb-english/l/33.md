## 第一部分 · 阅读理解 （第 1—15 题 · 建议 40 分钟）

共三篇文章。课件每题仅给出该题对应的原文段落，文章由各题所示段落按课件顺序拼合而成，括号内为课件所标段落位置（原样保留）；课件未展示的段落从略。

### Passage A（第 1—5 题）

（首段）With China’s rapid development in artificial intelligence (AI) technologies, more companies have noted the huge business potential in AI companionship sectors, as simulated AI pets with adorable appearances are gaining increasing popularity among Chinese consumers.

（第二段）Zhang Yi, CEO of the iiMedia Research Institute, said that consumers’ demand for emotional support and the capability of current AI technologies offer this type of product greater business potential.

（第三段）A 20-year-old student named Wang Li, who is also an AI technology fan, said that she bought “Boo Boo”, a simulated AI robotic pet developed by Hangzhou-based Genmoor Technology.

（第四段）“I first learned about the product during a major assignment investigation and remembered it,” said Wang Li.

（第五段）“I decided to buy it out of curiosity, but especially because of its adorable fluffy appearance and the color of its fur, which is just like my dog’s,” Wang Li added.

（课件未展示第六段，从略。）

（第七段）Wang Li stated that the advantage of having an AI pet is that it doesn’t require extra spending on food or time for walks every day, while noting that it still lacks interaction flexibility and real companionship when compared with real dogs or cats.

（末段）Zhang Yi predicted more possibilities in applying AI technologies in emotional support and companionship robots, with target users covering all age groups, and whose functions include the ability to detect users’ potential physical and mental health issues.

1. The huge business potential has been noticed by more companies in _____.
A. positive feedback
B. real pets like dogs or cats
C. AI companionship sectors
D. popularity among consumers
2. When did Wang Li learn about the simulated AI pets?
A. During her survey related to her major.
B. During her visit to Genmoor Technology.
C. During her work in iiMedia Research Institute.
D. During her surfing e-commerce platform Taobao.
3. Wang Li decided to buy Boo Boo for the following reasons EXCEPT _____.
A. her curiosity
B. the color of its fur
C. its lovely appearance
D. its emotional support
4. The disadvantage of having an AI pet is that _____.
A. it needs careful maintenance and power charge
B. it lacks interaction flexibility and real companionship
C. it can detect users’ potential physical and mental health issues
D. it requires no extra spending on food or time for walks every day
5. What’s the passage mainly about?
A. Simulated AI pets have huge business potential.
B. AI pets are applied in every aspect of life.
C. Simulated pets will sell well and be restocked frequently.
D. Boo Boo, a simulated AI robotic pet, is perfect without any disadvantages.

### Passage B（第 6—10 题）

（首段）Guochao, literally meaning “national tide”, refers to modern products packed with traditional Chinese cultural elements. In recent years, it has experienced a rise because of a growing interest among younger generations in their cultural heritage and a desire to integrate it into their daily lives. Many platforms now show traditional crafts, fashion and art, making them more accessible and appealing.

（第二段）For example, with the arrival of the year of the snake, the image of the snake is integrated into various products such as hongbao, the red envelope that older generations fill with money and give to young people as a symbol of good fortune…Guochao products combine traditional cultural elements with modern design.

（第三段）Shanxi’s cultural tourism has rapidly gained popularity because of the global hit online game Black Myth: Wukong. As many scenes in the game are created based on cultural and historical sites in Shanxi, the province is witnessing a remarkable surge in tourists, who are either the fans of the game or fans interested in the ancient architecture and sculptures and the stories behind them. The Guochao craze provides Shanxi with vast opportunities for innovation. The local government integrates Guochao elements into scenic area development and tourism souvenir creation. A line of refrigerator magnets shaped like the architecture in the game have been developed to increase the cultural value of tourism.

1. Guochao has experienced a rise because _____.
A. younger generations are fond of buying tourism souvenirs
B. younger generations are crazy about hongbao during the Spring Festival
C. younger generations are addicted to the online game Black Myth: Wukong
D. younger generations are interested in cultural heritage and eager to integrate it into their daily lives
2. What does the underlined word “appealing” mean in paragraph 1?
A. Supportive.
B. Protective.
C. Attractive.
D. Detective.
3. According to the passage, which is a proper description of hongbao in China?
A. It is a red envelope filled with notes and given to the young as a symbol of good wish.
B. It is a red envelope filled with gifts and given to the young as a symbol of good luck.
C. It is a red envelope filled with money and given to the young as a symbol of good fortune.
D. It is a red envelope filled with objects and given to the young as a symbol of good souvenirs.
4. Which is not TRUE about “Guochao” products?
A. They all have the theme of the snake.
B. They have modern design concepts.
C. They involve traditional cultural elements.
D. They can satisfy consumers’ pursuit of fashion.
5. It can be inferred from the last paragraph that _____.
A. the popularity of Shanxi’s cultural tourism lies in its commercials on media
B. the popularity of Shanxi’s cultural tourism is due to the online game Black Myth: Wukong
C. the popularity of Shanxi’s cultural tourism mainly owes to the hospitality of the local people
D. the popularity of Shanxi’s cultural tourism relies on its ancient architecture and sculptures

### Passage C（第 11—15 题）

（首段）As China’s visa-free travel policy continues to expand and facilitate easier exchanges between people, the attraction of “China Travel” becomes stronger.

（第二段）Francisco Lopez, a tourist from Spain, shared his experiences with the Global Times on Sunday, highlighting the ease of his trip to China made possible by the country’s visa-free policy and other supporting measures. During this trip, he visited Beijing, Xi’an, Guilin, and Shanghai… “We didn’t have cash and didn’t exchange any money. We used Alipay everywhere, and it worked perfectly,” he added.

（第三段）To meet the needs of foreign tourists, various regions and tourist destinations in China are enhancing the tourism experience. A worker from the Wulingyuan scenic area in Zhangjiajie of central China’s Hunan Province told the media that the visa-free policy has significantly boosted the tourism market, with a notable increase in the number of foreign visitors. “There is a shift in tourists, with more coming from southeast Asia and Europe thanks to the implementation of visa-free policies,” another employee said.

（第四段）In the first three quarters of this year, Zhangjiajie City received over 1.29 million inbound tourists, a year-on-year increase of 255.41 percent. The city achieved inbound tourism revenue of $358.54 million, marking a year-on-year growth of 307.61 percent, according to official data.

1. Francisco Lopez visited the following cities EXCEPT _____.
A. Beijing
B. Xi’an
C. Zhangjiajie
D. Guili
2. Why didn’t Francisco Lopez exchange any money during his trip to China?
A. Because he did not have cash.
B. Because he got everything for free.
C. Because China’s e-payment system is available everywhere.
D. Because China’s visa-free policy facilitates easier exchanges between people.
3. What does the underlined word “notable” mean in paragraph 3?
A. Reliable.
B. Remarkable.
C. Variable.
D. Terrible.
4. What can be inferred from this passage?
A. The visa-free policies will cause traffic jams in big cities.
B. The visa-free policies influenced Chinese people’s life.
C. The visa-free policies made the domestic tourists happy.
D. The visa-free policies brought about the growth of inbound tourists.
5. What is the main idea of this passage?
A. Tourist destinations in China enhance tourism experience.
B. More inbound tourists come from southeast Asia and Europe.
C. A Spanish tourist shared his experience of China’s e-payment system.
D. The visa-free policy has significantly boosted China’s tourism market.

**逐题解析**

**4. 答案 B**　与真宠物相比，其不足是互动灵活性和真实陪伴感不足。

**6. 答案 D**　国潮兴起源于年轻人关注文化遗产并希望融入日常生活。

**10. 答案 B**　山西文旅热度主要受《黑神话：悟空》带动。

**11. 答案 C**　列举访问城市不含 Zhangjiajie。

**12. 答案 C**　Alipay 到处可用，所以无需兑换现金。

**13. 答案 B**　notable 与 remarkable 同义，表示显著的。

**14. 答案 D**　数据表明免签政策推动入境游客与收入增长。

**15. 答案 D**　首尾均强调免签政策显著促进中国入境旅游市场。

## 第二部分 · 五选五 （第 16—20 题 · 建议 10 分钟）

**A Few Tips for Self-Acceptance**

We all want it to accept and love ourselves. But at times it seems too difficult and too far out of reach. __16__ Here’s a handful of ways that will set you in the right direction.

&bull; __17__ Do not follow the people who make you feel not-good-enough. Why do you follow them? Are you hoping that eventually you will feel empowered because your life is better than theirs? Know that your life is your own; you are the only you in this world.

&bull; Forgive yourself for mistakes that you have made. We are often ashamed of our shortcomings, our mistakes and our failures. __18__ You will make mistakes, time and time again. Rather than getting caught up in how you could have done better, why not offer yourself a compassionate（有同情心）response? “That didn’t go as planned. But, I tried my best.”

&bull; Recognize all of your strengths. Write them down in a journal. Begin to train your brain to look at strength before weakness. List all of your accomplishments and achievements. You have a job, earned your degree, and you got out of bed today. __19__

&bull; Now that you’ve listed your strengths, list your imperfections. Turn the page in your journal. Put into words why you feel unworthy, why you don’t feel good enough. Now, read these words back to yourself. __20__ Turn to a page in your journal to your list of strengths and achievements. See how awesome you are?

A. Feeling upset again?
B. Where do you start?
C. Nothing is too small to celebrate.
D. Remember, you are only human.
E. Stop comparing yourself with others.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

**逐题解析**

**16. 答案 B**　后文开始列举方法，Where do you start? 最自然。

**17. 答案 E**　该段劝人不要追随令自己自卑的人，对应停止比较。

**18. 答案 D**　犯错后应记住人非完人，对应 D。

**19. 答案 C**　连工作、起床等小成就也值得肯定，对应 C。

**20. 答案 A**　读完自我否定的话再回看优点，Feeling upset again? 承接恰当。

In those 8 months of unemployment, I’ve __22__ myself. I’ve smiled less. I’ve laughed less. My __23__ has supported me completely, which made him exhausted as well. At the end of the day, I sat in Union Square. I saw a man __28__ a “free hugs” sign and watched for a while as people came and __29__, some hugging him with smiles on their faces, some ignoring him __30__. I finished my coffee and decided, “I think I need a hug.”

I __31__ and hugged this man. I hugged him hard, and long, and tight. And I didn’t realize how badly I’d __32__ one until I started weeping. And after that __33__, I got on the train home and I spent time with my partner. And I started to try and __34__ everything I’d broken.

## 第四部分 · 语法填空 （第 36—45 题 · 建议 10 分钟）

The world is turning its eyes to Beijing on Wednesday, as China is staging a military parade（阅兵）to mark the 80th anniversary of the victory in the Chinese People’s War of Resistance Against Japanese Aggression and the World Anti-Fascist War.

This event carries deep political and historical meaning. Not only is it about __36__ (remember) past victories but it also honors the strong spirit and strength of the Chinese people. The parade shows national pride and pays respect to the many __37__ (hero) who __38__ (fight) for justice and peace. It displays China’s confidence and __39__ (ready) to face today’s challenges. It also reflects China’s promise to protect its sovereignty（主权）and territory（领土）, and its ability to help keep global peace.

Last Sunday and Monday, leaders from Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) member countries met in Tianjin, China. They issued __40__ joint statement marking the 80th anniversary of the victory over fascism and the __41__ (found) of the United Nations.

The statement recalled the great suffering（磨难）brought by fascism, __42__ took millions of lives and threatened freedom and peace worldwide. During the war, China resisted Japanese aggression for 14 years (1931-1945), with over 35 million casualties. Its people made enormous sacrifices.

However, China’s important role in defeating fascism is often overlooked in some Western accounts. In fact, China’s battlefields were a major part of the World Anti-Fascist War.

That’s __43__ it is important to remember this history. From its past experiences, China knows that peace requires strength. Today, China supports the United Nations and contributes actively to global peace and development.

China’s military parade is not __44__ (mean) to show off power or threaten others. __45__, it honors those who lost their lives in the war and shows China’s continued commitment to protecting world peace and fairness.

::: details 本节答案与解析（第 36—45 题）

答案速查（本节答案与解析为笔记整理时依据语法与语境核定的参考答案）：

| 36 | **remembering** | 37 | **heroes** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 38 | **fought** | 39 | **readiness** |
| 40 | **a** | 41 | **founding** |
| 42 | **which** | 43 | **why** |
| 44 | **meant** | 45 | **Instead** |

**逐题解析**

**36. 答案 remembering**　介词 about 后接动名词 remembering。考点：介词后接动名词（回炉 0011 课 · 非谓语）

**37. 答案 heroes**　many 后接复数 heroes。考点：many 修饰可数名词复数（回炉 0001 课 · 名词）

**38. 答案 fought**　先烈过去为正义而战，用过去式 fought。考点：一般过去时（回炉 0009 课 · 时态语态）

**39. 答案 readiness**　and 连接 confidence 与名词 readiness。

**40. 答案 a**　首次提及一份联合声明，用不定冠词 a。考点：不定冠词（回炉 0002 课 · 冠词）

**41. 答案 founding**　定冠词 the 后用名词 founding。

**42. 答案 which**　非限制性定语从句指代 fascism，用 which。考点：非限制性定语从句（回炉 0012 课 · 定语从句）

**43. 答案 why**　That’s why... 表示“那就是为什么”。

**44. 答案 meant**　be meant to do 表示“旨在”。

**45. 答案 Instead**　后句与炫耀武力相反，用 Instead。

:::

## 第五部分 · 写作 （第 46 题 · 建议 30 分钟）

46. Suppose you are Li Hua, a student at Hongxing College. Write a letter to apply for membership of the International Cultural Tourism Innovation Team. Your letter should include:
1) a brief self-introduction;
2) the reasons for your application.

::: details 本节答案与解析（第 46 题 · 参考范文）

I would be honored to be part of this inspiring community. I look forward to your reply and thank you for considering my application.

Yours sincerely,
Li Hua
