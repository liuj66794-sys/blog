题型技巧 · 第七课

约 100 词应用文，15 分——先审身份对象目的，再套「称呼—正文三段—落款」格式，最后逐项检查要点是否回应。

::: info 考点地图

1. **考情**：100 词应用文 15 分（教材上册 P230）；2015-2025 真题以**通知、邀请信、求职信**为主
2. **通用格式**：称呼（顶格）→ 正文三段（身份目的 / 叙事观点 / 总结期待）→ 落款（右下角）
3. **14 类模板与范文**：申请、投诉、道歉、建议、感谢、邀请、祝贺、慰问、求助、推荐、通知、演讲、倡议、邮件
4. **万能展开**：三段式句型库 + 四大万能理由 + 三大必备句型 + 分类型结尾

:::

::: danger 易错

使用边界模板是**结构支架**，不是可整段机械照抄的答案。考试时先圈出**身份、对象、目的、要点、时态与字数**，再替换模板中的占位内容；最终逐项检查题目要求是否全部回应。

:::

## 应用文通用格式（教材 P255；课件 p4-5）

| 结构 | 写法 |
| --- | --- |
| **1. 称呼** | 信的开头，**顶格写**。（1）写给机构：Dear sir or madam, / To Whom It May Concern,（2）写给个人：Dear Mr. Wang, / Dear Bob, / Dear Editors, |
| **2. 正文** | **第一段**：第 1 句自我介绍或打招呼；第 2 句写作目的（信的中心思想）。**第二段**：叙事 / 观点。**第三段**：第 1 句总结，第 2 句期待回信。 |
| **3. 落款** | 一般写在信的**右下角**。（1）结尾客套：Yours sincerely, / Yours truly, / Yours,（2）签名：如 Li Hua |

Dear Sir or Madam,

（正文）

Yours sincerely,
Wei Fang

译文：尊敬的先生或女士：……谨致问候，魏芳。

## 十四类应用文模板与范文

### 一 · 申请信 / 求职信（教材 P255）

Dear ____,

I am ____, currently ____（个人身份）. I am writing to formally apply for the position of ____. My decision to apply stems from ____.（申请的原因）

My qualifications align well with the requirements of this role. Firstly, I possess three years of hands-on experience in ____. Additionally, my background in ____ has equipped me with strong problem-solving skills. These competencies enable me to contribute effectively to ____（具体岗位）. The unique feature of the program/position really attracts me, and I am confident that I can grow and succeed in it.

I hope you can offer me an opportunity and thank you very much.

Yours faithfully,
（签名）

模板大意：本人目前（个人身份），现正式申请（职位名称）岗位；申请原因＋资历契合（三年实战经验、解决问题能力），期待贵方给予机会，谨此致谢。

**【2023 广东真题】**假设你是李华，你得知某英文报社正在招聘兼职记者，你有意应聘该职位。请你写一封求职信，内容应包括：(1) 表明对该职位感兴趣；(2) 陈述你的优势；(3) 希望得到回复。

Dear Madam or Sir,

I am writing to apply for the position of part-time reporter.

To be frank, it is a great honor for me to introduce myself briefly. There are several strengths that can cater to the qualities required of the candidates applying for the position, which is the reason why I get interested in it.（有几个优势可以迎合申请职位的候选人所需的素质，这就是我对它感兴趣的原因。）To start with, I am a boy/ a girl named Li Hua who is optimistic and confident. It is such positive characters that enable me to establish favorable interpersonal relationships with others, which will exert a positive effect on my future work. In addition, I specialized in/ major in International trade. Often Involved in international communication, I have a good command of English, especially oral English, making it easy for me to communicate with foreigners in English. What matters most is that I always keep everything in order, which is key to taking on the position.

In a nutshell, should you grant me a personal interview, I would be most grateful.

Sincerely yours,
Li Hua

::: details 范文译文（2023 真题）

尊敬的女士或先生：我写信申请兼职记者职位。坦白说，能有机会向您做个自我介绍，我深感荣幸。这份工作要求的素质与我的优势高度契合，正是这份契合让我格外关注。我叫李华，性格乐观自信。正是这种积极向上的特质让我能与人建立良好关系，这对未来工作大有裨益。另外，我专攻国际贸易专业，长期参与国际交流，英语水平尤其突出，口语能力过硬，能轻松与外国人用英语沟通。最重要的是我做事有条不紊，这正是胜任这份工作的关键。总之，若您能安排面试，我将不胜感激。——李华

:::

### 二 · 投诉信（教材 P257）

To Whom It May Concern,

I am ____（表明身份）. Here I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with ____（具体事件）.

The reasons are as follows. In the first place, ____（问题1）. What's more, ____（问题2）. Under these circumstances, I find that ____（问题所带来的后果）. I hope you could ____（提出请求）, and I would like to have this matter settled by ____（设定解决事件的最后期限）.

Thank you for your consideration and your time. I am looking forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely,
（签名）

模板大意：本人（身份）谨此致函，就（具体事件）表达不满；首先（问题1）、其次（问题2）、后果如何；恳请贵方（请求），希望（期限）前解决；感谢关注，期待回复。

**【2017 广东真题】**假设你是李明，最近参加旅行社（travel agency）组织的一次旅游，但你对此次旅行很不满意。请你向客服（customer service）写一封投诉信，阐述投诉的原因（如：酒店、饮食、交通及费用等）并提出你期待的解决办法。

Dear Customer Service,

My name is Li Ming and I am a customer of your travel agency. I'm writing to express my dissatisfaction with the service of your travel agency during my recent travel.

First of all, during the trip the tour guide took us to many different places to go shopping which cost us too much money and time. In addition, most of us thought that the guide was not competent enough because he knew little about the scenic spots. What was worse, the food that was served to us was of poor quality and the accommodation conditions were rather awful. At last, the provided transportation was not convenient at all, which wasted us a lot of time.

I hope that you can take all the above into consideration and make up for our losses. Moreover, it's high time that you should take some measures to improve your service.

Your sincerely,
Li Ming

::: details 范文译文（2017 真题）

亲爱的客服：我叫李明，是贵旅行社的客户。我写这封信是为了表达我对贵旅行社在我近期旅行期间所提供服务的不满。首先，在旅行期间，导游带我们去了很多不同的地方购物，这花费了我们太多的金钱和时间。此外，我们大多数人认为这位导游不够称职，因为他对景点知之甚少。更糟糕的是，给我们提供的食物质量很差，住宿条件也相当糟糕。最后，提供的交通一点也不方便，浪费了我们很多时间。我希望您能考虑到以上所有情况，并弥补我们的损失。此外，现在是您采取一些措施来改善服务的时候了。——李明

:::

### 三 · 道歉信（教材 P258-259）

Dear ____,

I am very sorry that ____. This letter serves to convey my heartfelt remorse and provide clarity on the circumstances.

I will be very grateful if you are kind to listen to my detailed explanation. Due to ____（具体原因）, ____（事件）unfortunately took place. Consequently, ____.（造成的后果）I do hope you can understand me. Would you please accept my apology? I would appreciate your allowing me to ____.（解决措施）

Please accept my profound apologies once more. Your understanding and patience during this resolution process would mean a great deal to me.

Yours sincerely,
（签名）

模板大意：深感抱歉，此信表达诚挚歉意并说明情况；由于（原因），（事件）不幸发生，造成（后果）；希望得到理解，并请求允许（解决措施）；再次郑重致歉。

**练习题干**：假定你是李华，你的英国朋友 John 与你约好这周末去远足，你临时接到学校通知要参加戏剧节演出的排练，不能如期赴约。写一封 100 词的道歉信。请根据以下要点给 John 写封邮件，内容包括：表达歉意；不能赴约的原因；解决措施。

Dear John,

I'm writing to sincerely apologize for canceling our hiking trip this weekend. Unexpectedly, my school has scheduled urgent rehearsals for the drama festival.

I was genuinely excited about our adventure and feel terrible for letting you down. To make amends（为了弥补过失）, could we reschedule for next Sunday? I'll plan the route and prepare snacks to ensure a perfect day.

Please forgive this last-minute change. Your understanding would mean the world to me. Let's stay in touch!

Yours sincerely,
Li Hua

::: details 范文译文（道歉信练习）

我写这封信是想诚恳地为取消本周末的徒步旅行道歉。没想到学校突然安排了戏剧节的紧急彩排。我真的对这次冒险充满期待，现在很抱歉让大家失望了。为了弥补，能否把行程改到下个星期天？我会提前规划路线并准备零食，确保大家度过完美的一天。请原谅这临时的改变，你的理解对我来说意义重大。咱们保持联系吧！

:::

### 四 · 建议信（教材 P260）

Dear ____,

I have received your letter saying that you ____. However, the situation is easy to change if you take my advice. Here are some tips to help you.

To begin with, ____.（建议1）In addition, it might be a viable（切实可行的）option if you ____.（建议2）I am sure ____.（对所提建议的效果表示肯定）

If these suggestions are followed, you will surely improve the current situation. I am eagerly looking forward to your response.（若采纳这些建议，现状必将得到改善。期待您的回复。）

Yours sincerely,
（签名）

**【2014 广东真题】**你叫张新，与李明是好朋友，在不同大学读书。你得知他沉迷于（indulge in）玩网络游戏（online game-playing），影响了学习，期末考试有几门课程不及格。请给他写封信，说明沉迷于网游的危害，劝他珍惜大学时光，学好专业知识。
温馨提示：不可将上述内容直接翻译成英语；信中不能出现考生的真实姓名与学校名。

Dear Li Ming,

I have received your letter saying that you've been struggling with your studies recently. However, the situation is easy to change if you take my advice. Here are some tips to help you.

To begin with, try to limit your online game time and make a detailed study schedule to focus on your major courses. In addition, it might be a viable option if you join study groups or campus activities to shift your attention from games. I am sure these efforts will help you catch up with your classmates soon.

If these suggestions are followed, you will surely improve the current situation. I am eagerly looking forward to your response.

Yours sincerely,
Zhang Xin

::: details 范文译文（2014 真题）

我收到您来信反映最近学习状态不佳。不过只要听从我的建议，情况完全能扭转。这里有几个实用建议：首先，建议您控制游戏时间，制定详细学习计划专注主修课程；其次，加入学习小组或校园社团活动，通过集体学习转移注意力，这些方法都值得尝试。相信这些方法能帮助您尽快赶上同学进度。只要坚持这些建议，你的学习状态定会有所改善。期待您的回复，祝好！

:::

### 五 · 感谢信（教材 P262）

Dear ____,

I would like to convey in this letter my heartfelt thanks to you for ____.（感谢的原因）

During ____, you give me a lot of help. It is your kindness and help that have enabled me to get out of the difficult situation / solved my problem. Now I would like to take this opportunity to express my great appreciation for your timely support and assistance.

I have no words to thank you enough. Please accept my gratitude.

Yours sincerely,
（签名）

模板大意：谨此致谢，衷心感谢您在（原因）期间给予的大力支持；正是您的鼎力相助，才让我摆脱困境、化解难题；请接受我最诚挚的谢意。

**练习题干**：假如你是李华，最近你作为交换生去纽约参加了英语夏令营，其间住在 Green 夫妇家里。他们为你提供了吃住等方面的帮助。请写一封 100 词感谢信，内容包括以下要点：表达感谢；回顾难忘的经历；发出邀请。

Dear Mr. and Mrs. Green,

I'm writing to express my heartfelt gratitude for your warm hospitality during my stay in New York.

The cozy room and delicious homemade meals (especially those blueberry pancakes!) made me feel at home. The trip to Central Park and the rooftop barbecue night will forever be cherished memories. Your stories about American culture deepened my understanding beyond textbooks.

I'd be honored to host you in China someday. Let me show you the Great Wall and my hometown's ancient temples. Please let me know if you plan to visit!

With warmest regards,
Li Hua

::: details 范文译文（感谢信练习）

谨此致谢，衷心感谢您在纽约期间的款待。温馨的客房与家常美味（尤其是蓝莓煎饼！）让我倍感宾至如归。中央公园的漫步与屋顶烧烤之夜，将永远珍藏于心。您讲述的美国文化故事，让我对这片土地的理解远超课本知识。若有机会，我愿在中国为您举办招待会。期待带您领略长城风采，探访家乡古刹。若有来华计划，还望不吝告知！

:::

### 六 · 邀请信（教材 P263）

Dear ____,

I'm ____.（表明身份）It is with the greatest pleasure that I write this letter to invite you to ____.

There will be a(n) ____（活动名称）at/in ____（地点）on ____（时间）. And this event aims to... The details about the activity are as follows. First, ____.（活动一）Second, ____.（活动二）Additionally, ____.（活动三）I truly hope you can accept this invitation as your presence will add more color and joy to the event.

Please let me know your decision at your earliest convenience. Looking forward to seeing you!

Yours sincerely,
（签名）

模板大意：非常荣幸致信邀请您参加（活动）；活动于（日期）（时间）（地点）举行，旨在……具体安排如下（活动一二三）；您的到来将为活动增添精彩，请尽快告知决定。

**【2024 年广东真题】**你是李华，你校英语报在为外国交换生开展名为「我眼中的中国」征文比赛，请你给你校交换生 Mark 写一封电子邮件，邀请他参赛，内容如下：1. 活动介绍；2. 征文要求；3. 邀请参赛。
参考词汇：writing contest（征文比赛）。注意：正文内容不少于 100 词，不得出现真实姓名和学校等信息。

::: info

评分标准信息完整，语言规范，语篇连贯。（2024 年广东真题评分标准）

:::

Dear Mark,

Knowing that you show interest in Chinese culture, I'm writing to invite you to a writing contest designed for exchange students in our school.

First and foremost, the contest, whose theme is "China in my eyes", will be held by the English Newspaper of our school in our school library on April 18th, 2024. Besides, the contestants are supposed to write an essay of about two hundred words with regard to "what appeals to you most about Chinese traditional culture", "what kind of Chinese food is your favorite", and so on. Those writings should be submitted before April 10th, 2024.

Since you have made great progress in learning Chinese, why not take part in this activity? I'm sure that you will have a good performance in the contest. Looking forward to your early reply!

Yours sincerely,
Li Hua

::: details 范文译文（2024 真题）

亲爱的马克：看到您对中国文化感兴趣，特此邀请您参加我校为交换生举办的写作比赛。本次以「我眼中的中国」为主题的征文活动，将于 2024 年 4 月 18 日在图书馆举行，由校报主办。参赛者需撰写约 200 字的作文，主题可围绕「最吸引您的中国传统文化元素」「最钟爱的中国美食」等展开。作品请于 2024 年 4 月 10 日前提交。鉴于您在中文学习上已取得显著进步，何不借此机会参与其中？相信您定能交出亮眼答卷。期待您的佳作！——李华

:::

### 七 · 祝贺信（教材 P265）

Dear ____,

I have learned with delight that you ____. Please allow me to express my hearty congratulations and I feel very happy for you.

You are really ____.（对祝贺人的评价）I know this is surely owing to your hard work and dedication. The countless hours you spent in the ____（领域）have clearly been well-invested. This achievement not only shows your intelligence but also your determination.

With your ____, I am sure that you will be a ____.（对祝贺人的信心）Please accept our sincerest congratulations and very best wishes for all the good future.

Yours sincerely,
（签名）

模板大意：欣喜获悉您取得的成就，致以最诚挚的祝贺；这离不开您的辛勤付出，成就彰显智慧与决心；相信凭您的（……），您必将成为（……）；祝未来一切顺利。

**【2011 广东真题】**你叫李明，去年考上了大学，而你的同桌张强却落榜了。经过一年的复习，他今年也考上了大学。你得知此消息后，十分高兴，给他写了一封贺信。信中谈到了你们三年高中学习中难忘的事情以及你们之间的友谊，还可以向他简单谈谈你的大学生活感受，并且给他一些忠告，以应付即将开始的大学生活。
温馨提示：你只能根据上述提示写一封贺信，不可以直接将上面这段文字翻译成英语。不可以署你的真实姓名。

Dear Zhang Qiang,

I have learned with delight that you have been admitted to university this year. Please allow me to express my hearty congratulations and I feel very happy for you.

You are really persistent and determined. I know this is surely owing to your hard work and dedication. The countless hours you spent in the review classroom have clearly been well-invested. I still remember how we stayed up late preparing for exams in high school—those days forged our precious friendship. My college life is fulfilling but requires self-discipline; try to balance study and activities.

With your perseverance, I am sure that you will be a top student in your major. Please accept my sincerest congratulations and very best wishes for a brilliant future.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

::: details 范文译文（2011 真题）

张强同学：得知您今年顺利考入大学，我由衷感到欣喜。在此向您致以最诚挚的祝贺，衷心祝愿您前程似锦。您展现出的执着与毅力，正是源于日复一日的刻苦钻研。那些在自习室度过的无数个夜晚，都已化作宝贵的知识财富。还记得高中时我们熬夜备考的场景吗？正是这些共同的奋斗时光，铸就了我们珍贵的友谊。大学生活虽然充满挑战，但更需要自律自强，希望您能平衡学业与课外活动。相信凭着这份坚持，您定能成为专业领域的佼佼者。在此送上最真挚的祝福，愿您未来大放异彩。——李明

:::

### 八 · 慰问信（教材 P266）

Dear ____,

I was deeply concerned to learn about ____（困扰事件）. Please know that you're not alone in this, and your courage to face difficulties is truly admirable.

Every effort you've made—whether staying up late to study or balancing work and courses—has strengthened your resilience. I know you're feeling down now, but we also need to learn from this failure, identify the reasons why we failed and make corrections.

This temporary hurdle does not define your capabilities. With your determination, I'm confident brighter days lie ahead. Let's meet this weekend to brainstorm solutions over coffee—my treat!

Yours sincerely,
（签名）

模板大意：得知你遭遇（困扰事件）深感关切，你并非孤军奋战；你的努力让你更加坚韧，要从挫折中汲取教训；这次小插曲不能否定你的能力，相信未来会更好。

### 练习一 · 给生病的网友提出健康建议（题海 P92）

**题目**：假定你是李华。你的网友 David 是一名正在中国学习的交换生，他最近身体不适，经常感冒，并已影响学习。请给他写一封电子邮件。内容包括：1. 说明饮食和运动的重要性；2. 提出具体建议；3. 表达祝愿。写作要求：采用应用文基本格式；词数不少于 100 词。

**写作拆解与可用句型**：

- 开头（问候并点明事由，先表达关心，再引出「身体不好影响学习」）：I'm really sorry to hear that you've been feeling unwell lately—you often catch colds, and this has even affected your studies.（很遗憾听说你最近身体不适——你经常感冒，甚至已经影响了学习。）
- 正文第一层（饮食和运动的重要性，并与对抗感冒、恢复状态相联系）：Keeping a balanced diet and doing regular exercise are super vital for your health right now.（目前，保持均衡饮食并经常锻炼对你的健康极其重要。）
- 正文第二层·饮食建议：Try to eat more fresh fruits (like oranges—they have vitamin C!) and vegetables instead of junk food.（尽量多吃新鲜水果和蔬菜，而不要吃垃圾食品。）
- 正文第二层·运动建议：You should take a 20-minute walk after class every day, or do some simple stretches in your dorm. It's easy but helps you stay energetic.（你应该每天课后散步 20 分钟，或者在宿舍做一些简单的拉伸。这很容易做到，却能帮助你保持精力充沛。）
- 结尾（希望对方康复并尽快跟上学习）：I truly hope you'll follow these small tips. I'm sure you'll feel better soon and catch up with your studies quickly.（我真心希望你能采纳这些小建议。我相信你很快就会好起来，并迅速赶上学习进度。）

Wish you all the best.

Yours,
Li Hua

**题目**：假定你是李华，暑假期间正在伦敦学习。当地一家美术馆即将举办中国画展览，请给美术馆写一封信申请做志愿者。内容包括：1. 写信目的；2. 个人优势；3. 你能做的事情。写作要求：词数不少于 100 词。

**写作拆解与可用句型**：

- 开头（说明信息来源和申请意愿）：I am writing to apply for the volunteer position at the upcoming Chinese Painting Exhibition hosted by your art gallery, which I learned about recently during my summer study in London.（我写信是想申请贵画廊即将举办的中国画展的志愿者岗位；我最近在伦敦暑期学习期间得知了这一消息。）
- 正文·文化优势：As a Chinese student who has studied traditional Chinese painting for 5 years, I have a deep understanding of the cultural connotations behind artworks.（作为一名学习中国传统绘画已有五年的中国学生，我深刻理解艺术作品背后的文化内涵。）
- 正文·语言优势：Fluent in both Chinese and English, I can smoothly bridge the communication gap between potential Chinese contributors and local visitors.（我中英文流利，能够顺畅弥合潜在中国参展者与当地观众之间的沟通差距。）
- 正文·经验优势：I also have previous volunteer experience at a cultural exhibition in my hometown, where I guided visitors and introduced artworks—this has equipped me with the basic skills for exhibition volunteer work.（我还曾在家乡的一场文化展览中担任志愿者，负责引导观众和介绍艺术作品；这段经历使我具备了展览志愿服务所需的基本技能。）
- 能做的事情（结合画展场景列出可实际承担的任务）：During the exhibition, I can guide visitors around the gallery and explain the cultural backgrounds, artistic styles of each Chinese painting in detail (in both English and Chinese).（展览期间，我可以带领观众参观画廊，并用中英文详细讲解每幅中国画的文化背景和艺术风格。）

::: info

课件提示文中的所有优势都要围绕「中国画展」展开；语气要正式、礼貌。

:::

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am a Chinese student named Li Hua, who is studying in London during the summer vacation. Knowing that the local art gallery will hold an exhibition of Chinese paintings, I am writing to apply for volunteering. As a student who has been learning Chinese painting since 5 years old, I am very pleased to have an opportunity to introduce Chinese paintings to foreigners. Besides, my fluent spoken English and rich knowledge about Chinese paintings will help visitors understand the meaning behind each painting deeply. I would appreciate it if I could be given the opportunity.

Looking forward to your early reply.

Yours,
Li Hua

### 练习五 · 因受伤不能参加植物园活动而道歉（题海 P96）

**题目**：假定你是李华。你原计划本周末和几名国际学生一起参观植物园（botanical garden），但因意外受伤不能参加。请给活动组织者 John 写一封电子邮件表示歉意。内容包括：1. 表达自己原本对活动的期待；2. 说明不能参加的原因；3. 表达歉意。写作要求：词数不少于 100 词。

**写作拆解与可用句型**：

- 开头（表达期待，再用转折引出意外）：I've been really looking forward to the botanical garden visit this weekend. Unfortunately, I had an accident yesterday.（我一直非常期待本周末参观植物园。不幸的是，我昨天出了意外。）
- 正文（清楚交代意外、伤情、医嘱及其对行程的影响）：I fell off my bike on the way to the supermarket and sprained my ankle badly. The doctor examined it and advised me to rest in bed for at least 3 days, so I can't leave the house this weekend.（我在骑自行车去超市的路上摔了下来，脚踝严重扭伤。医生检查后建议我至少卧床休息三天，所以这个周末我无法出门。）
- 结尾（真诚致歉，同时顾及对方为组织活动所作的努力）：I feel truly sorry for letting you down—I know you've put a lot of effort into organizing this trip, and my absence might bring some inconvenience. I'm really apologetic for missing the activity with everyone.（我真的很抱歉让你失望了。我知道你为组织这次活动付出了很多努力，而我的缺席可能会带来一些不便。没能和大家一起参加活动，我深感歉意。）

Dear John,

I'm writing to apologize for not being able to go to the botanical garden with you this weekend.

You know, I have been expecting to go with you because I think it is a good chance to get close to nature and relax ourselves as well. It is also a good opportunity for us to know more about each other and strengthen our friendship. However, my ankle got seriously hurt while I was having PE class. The doctor told me to have a good rest and not to move at the moment. That is why I have to cancel my plan.

Sorry for not being able to go with you. Wish you all have a good time.

Yours,
Li Hua

### 练习六 · 感谢住院期间给予帮助的师生（题海 P97）

**题目**：假定你是李华。你住院期间，老师和同学前来看望，并帮助你补上落下的课程。请给老师和同学写一封感谢信。内容包括：1. 表明写信目的；2. 说明这些帮助给你带来的感受和影响。写作要求：词数不少于 100 词。

**写作拆解与可用句型**：

- 开头（感谢老师和同学在住院期间前来看望并帮助补课）：I'm writing this letter to express my deepest thanks to all of you—your kindness during my hospital stay made me feel warm and supported when I was feeling unwell.（我写这封信是为了向你们所有人表达最深切的谢意——住院期间，在我身体不适时，你们的善意让我感到温暖并得到了支持。）
- 正文·情绪上的安慰：When you came to the hospital with fresh fruits and your class notes, I felt instantly less lonely and anxious.（当你们带着新鲜水果和课堂笔记来医院看我时，我立刻不再那么孤独和焦虑了。）
- 正文·补课带来的帮助：Classmates help me make up for the missed lessons also meant a great deal to me—I didn't fall behind the class because of my absence, which relieved my worries about my studies.（同学们帮助我补上落下的课程，对我而言也意义重大——我没有因为缺课而落后，这缓解了我对学业的担忧。）

::: danger 易错

原文语言点课件原句以 Classmates help me... 开头，存在所有格/动名词搭配问题。正式写作可改为：**My classmates' help in making up for the missed lessons** also meant a great deal to me.（同学们在帮我补课方面给予的帮助也对我意义重大。）

:::

With your help, I didn't fall behind in my studies and even felt more confident about catching up when I return to school.

Thank you again for your kindness. I'm looking forward to being back with you soon.

Yours,
Li Hua

## 万能展开 · 从开头到结尾的句型库（202603 版 p48-72）

### 1. 三段式书信结构

| 部分 | 内容 |
| --- | --- |
| 称呼 | Dear ______,（注意后面有逗号） |
| 第一段 | 表明身份（可有可无）＋表明主题（写信目的） |
| 第二段 | 展开主题＋两点论（或三点论） |
| 第三段 | 重申主题＋寒暄套话（可有可无） |
| 落款 | Yours sincerely,（注意后面有逗号）＋ Li Ming（注意姓名后没有逗号） |

### 2. 第一段：表明身份与主题

**第一句·表明身份**，基本结构 **I am ...**（我是……）：

- 学生类：an undergraduate / a graduate majoring in ＋ 专业（一名主修……专业的本科生／研究生）；a freshman / sophomore / junior / senior（一名大一／大二／大三／大四学生）。
- 其他身份：a roommate / reader / customer / classmate / fan（一名室友／读者／顾客／同学／粉丝）。
- 万能身份：**I am president of the Students' Union.**（我是学生会主席。）

**第二句·表明主题**：**I am writing to do sth.**（我写信是为了做某事，可抄题干，也可自行表达）；**I am writing the letter to you for the purpose of doing sth.**（我给您写这封信，目的是做某事。）

### 3. 不同写信目的：标志词与参考句式

| 目的 | 标志词与同义替换 | 参考句式 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **建议** | suggest；advice / suggestion / proposal / opinion / idea | 1. It is my great pleasure to offer you some advice on ... 2. Here are some suggestions about ... for your reference. 3. In response to your request, I would like to propose that ... / suggest you do ... |
| **感谢** | thank；thanks / gratitude / appreciation | 1. I am sincerely grateful to you for your timely help. 2. Thank you so much for your generous hospitality. 3. May I take this opportunity to express my heartfelt thanks to you? |
| **投诉** | complain；complaint / dissatisfaction / indignation | 1. I am really annoyed at doing ... 2. I have to draw your attention to the problem / fault of ... 3. I am most reluctant to complain, but I must say that ＋（投诉事由） |
| **推荐** | recommend；recommendation / introduction / introduce | 1. I strongly / highly recommend ... 2. I take great pleasure in recommending to you ... 3. When it comes to ..., I shall, without any hesitation, recommend ... |
| **邀请** | invite；invitation | 1. May I have the honor to invite you to ...? 2. On behalf of ..., I would like to invite you to ... 3. We hereby extend our cordial invitation to you to ... |
| **道歉** | apology；regret / sorry | 1. I am indeed very sorry for ... / that ＋（从句） 2. Please accept my sincere apology that ＋（从句） 3. I am writing to express my regret / my apology that ＋（从句） |
| **求职** | apply for；qualified / candidate / submit / application / want to | 1. I would like to apply for the vacancy of ... 2. Please consider me for your advertised position of ... 3. I am writing this letter to apply for the position that you have advertised. |
| **询问** | inquire；ask for / wonder / inquiry | 1. The letter is to inquire about the details of ... 2. I would be most grateful if you could send me information regarding / concerning ... 3. I would like to know whether you could provide me with information regarding / concerning ... |
| **辞职 / 申请 / 倡议** | 辞职 resign（quit / leave / resignation）；申请 apply for（同求职）；倡议 advocate（encourage / inspire / call for） | ——（标志词记忆即可） |
| **祝贺** | congratulate；congratulation | 1. I offer you my warmest congratulations on ... 2. Let me offer my sincerest congratulations upon ... 3. I would like to congratulate you and share the pleasure with you. |
| **拒绝 / 慰问 / 答复** | 拒绝 decline（cannot accept / refuse / refusal）；慰问 comfort（sorry to hear）；答复 reply（in response to your last letter） | In response to your last letter, I would like to take this great opportunity to express my heartfelt appreciation for your inquiry of ... |

### 4. 第二段：段首句与过渡词

**段首句（四选一）**：

1. **Of all the points / suggestions / details concerned, I'd like to name some significant ones as follows.**（在所有有关的要点／建议／细节中，我想列举如下要点。）
2. **The following reasons / proposals / details are worth mentioning.**（值得一提的是以下原因／建议／细节。）
3. **The following reasons / proposals / details cannot be neglected.**（以下原因／建议／细节不容忽视。）
4. **Listed below is the relevant information.**（相关信息如下。）

**段内过渡词**：首先 first and foremost / in the first place / to start with / to begin with；其次 plus / in the second place / in addition / additionally / furthermore / moreover；最后 finally / lastly / more importantly / last but not least。

### 5. 第二段具体填充：万能词条

- **地点与时间**：The event will be held / The activity will be organized at the auditorium / on the playground of our university from 9:00 a.m. to 11:30 a.m. on March 10th, 2026.（本次活动于上午 9 点至 11 点 30 分在我校礼堂／操场举行。）

::: danger 易错

课件原文核查此句英文写的是 March 10th, 2026，课件中文译文写的是「2026 年 3 月 28 日」，日期不一致；使用时需统一。

:::

- **合作**：Cooperation consciousness, under any circumstances, tends to be of great importance because it can greatly enhance our work efficiency.（合作意识在任何情况下都非常重要，因为它可以大大提高我们的工作效率。）也可接 because it can exert positive influence on our work / life.（对工作／生活产生积极影响。）
- **掌握英语／汉语**：You should be good at / be proficient in English / Chinese in order to communicate easily / enhance work efficiency. 或 You should have a good grasp of / a good command of English / Chinese in order to communicate easily / enhance work efficiency.（为了方便沟通／提高工作效率，你需要精通英语／汉语。）
- **活动**：The cultural events will be held, which enable you to acquire adequate historical and cultural knowledge.（将举行文化活动，使你获得足够的历史和文化知识。）The sports events will be held, which exert positive influence on our health both mentally and physically.（将举行体育赛事，对身心健康都有积极影响。）
- **万能景点（以 the Great Wall and Beihai Park 为例）**：Firstly, they have a long history, which enables you to acquire adequate historical and cultural knowledge.（首先，它们有悠久的历史，使你能够获得足够的历史文化知识。）Secondly, it is generally recognized that climbing the Great Wall exerts positive influence on our health both mentally and physically.（其次，人们普遍认为爬长城对我们的身心健康都有积极的影响。）

### 6. 四大万能理由

| 角度 | 核心词 | 常用表达 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **一 · 成长、成功、发展** | growth / success / development | the growth / success / prospect of individuals（个人的成长／成功／前景）；the development of economy / society / culture / legal system / environment（经济／社会／文化／法制／环境的发展）；cultural / family / school environment（文化／家庭／校园环境） |
| **二 · 知识、阅历、机会** | knowledge / experience / opportunity | acquire adequate knowledge and experience（获得足够的知识和经验）；adequate knowledge 可替换为 useful / extensive experience（有用的／丰富的经验）、golden opportunity（宝贵机会） |
| **三 · 效率、便利、时间** | efficiency / convenience / time | enhance our work / study efficiency（提高工作／学习效率，enhance 可替换为 improve / develop / increase）；bring / offer great / tremendous convenience in our life（给生活带来巨大便利）；saving our precious time（节省宝贵时间）／a total waste of our precious time（完全浪费宝贵时间） |
| **四 · 健康、压力、愉悦** | health / pressure / pleasure | pressure / stress / strain（压力）；relieve / moderate physical and mental stress（缓解身心压力）；give rise to / cause a lot of mental and physical stress（产生大量身心压力）；bring a lot of mental and physical pleasure（给身心带来极大愉悦） |

### 7. 四大理由必备句型

**句型一 · 最高级价值**：**It is generally recognized that nothing is more important than（主题词）for ...**（人们普遍认为，（主题词）对……最重要。）注意：主题词均为名词性，即 more important than ＋ 名词／doing sth.，for ＋ 名词／doing sth.。

It is generally recognized that nothing is more important than reading for acquiring useful / extensive / good experience.
人们普遍认为，读书对获取有用的／大量的／良好的经验十分重要。

It is generally recognized that nothing is more important than AI for saving our precious time and money.
人们普遍认为，AI 对节约我们宝贵的时间和金钱十分重要。

**句型二 · 有助于**：**（主题词）contributes to / is conducive to / is responsible for ...**（（主题词）有助于／促成／是……的原因。）主题词均为名词性内容。

Participating in activities is conducive to the growth of individuals.
参加活动有助于个体的成长。

Exercise is conducive to relieving / moderating physical and mental stress.
锻炼有助于缓解身心压力。

**句型三 · 产生影响**：**（主题词）exerts a / an positive / profound / far-reaching influence / effect / impact on ...**（（主题词）对……产生积极的／深远的影响。）主题词使用名词性内容。

Technology exerts positive influence on the development of economy.
技术对经济的发展产生了积极的影响。

The Internet exerts positive influence on efficient learning / studying.
网络对高效学习产生了积极的影响。

### 8. 第三段与分类型结尾

**第三段**为非重点段落，只需重申主题；若有必要，配上一两句寒暄套话即可：**Please accept my sincere gratitude to you for reading the content above. If you need to know more about it, please contact me.**（衷心感谢您阅读以上内容。如果您需要了解更多信息，请与我联系。）

| 类型 | 结尾句 |
| --- | --- |
| **建议信** | 1. I hope you will take my suggestions into account. 2. It is my earnest hope that my suggestions can be accepted. 3. I sincerely hope that these proposals are useful for your decision-making. |
| **邀请信** | 1. I am looking forward to seeing you. 2. Please confirm your participation at your earliest convenience. 3. We would appreciate it if you could honor us with your presence. |
| **道歉信** | 1. I am sorry for causing you inconvenience. 2. I hope you will forgive my mistake which I terribly regret. 3. I sincerely hope that you will be able to think in my position and accept my apology. |
| **求职信** | 1. I shall be much obliged if you accord me an opportunity for an interview. 2. I hope that you will be kind enough to consider my application favorably. 3. Should you give me a trial, I would do my utmost to make your decision worthwhile. |
| **询问信（期待回信）** | 1. Your prompt attention to this letter would be highly appreciated. 2. I am looking forward to your prompt reply to this letter. 3. I am looking forward to your due attention and quick response. |
| **祝贺信** | 1. We wish to share your honor and joy. 2. I wish you success and fulfillment in the future. 3. All my best wishes for an even more prosperous future. |
| **保持联系／祝好** | 1. Should there be anything I can do for you, please contact me without hesitation. 2. If you need to know more about it, please contact me. |

### 第二组 · 开头句型与写信目的（标志词判断）

**第一段第一句「表明身份」的万能句是哪句？**

- A. I am writing to do sth.
- B. I am president of the Students’ Union.
- C. How is everything going?
- D. Listed below is the relevant information.

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（I am president of the Students’ Union.）** —— 笔记补充解析（万能展开 · 表明身份）：基本结构 I am ...；万能身份 I am president of the Students’ Union.（我是学生会主席）。

:::

**第一段第二句「表明主题」常用哪个句型？**

- A. I am writing to do sth.（可抄题干，也可自行表达）
- B. I am a freshman.
- C. It is my great pleasure to offer you some advice.
- D. Yours sincerely,

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：A（I am writing to do sth.（可抄题干，也可自行表达））** —— 笔记补充解析（万能展开 · 表明主题）：I am writing to do sth. 我写信是为了做某事；或 I am writing the letter to you for the purpose of doing sth.。

:::

**写建议信开头，该选哪句？**

**答案：B（It is my great pleasure to offer you some advice on ...）** —— 笔记补充解析（标志词与参考句式）：建议的标志词 suggest，同义替换 advice / suggestion / proposal / opinion / idea。

:::

**写感谢信开头，该选哪句？**

- A. I am sincerely grateful to you for your timely help.
- B. I have to draw your attention to the problem of ...
- C. I would like to apply for the vacancy of ...
- D. Please accept my sincere apology that ...

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：A（I am sincerely grateful to you for your timely help.）** —— 笔记补充解析（标志词与参考句式）：感谢的标志词 thank，同义替换 thanks / gratitude / appreciation。

:::

**写投诉信开头，该选哪句？**

- B. I take great pleasure in recommending to you ...

:::

**写推荐信开头，该选哪句？**

- A. When it comes to ..., I shall, without any hesitation, recommend ...
- B. I am indeed very sorry for ...
- C. The letter is to inquire about the details of ...
- D. I am really annoyed at doing ...

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：A（When it comes to ..., I shall, without any hesitation, recommend ...）** —— 笔记补充解析（标志词与参考句式）：推荐的标志词 recommend，同义替换 recommendation / introduction / introduce。

:::

**写邀请信开头，该选哪句？**

- A. I would like to apply for the vacancy of ...
- B. May I take this opportunity to express my heartfelt thanks?
- C. I am writing to express my regret that ...
- D. May I have the honor to invite you to ...?

**答案：D（May I have the honor to invite you to ...?）** —— 笔记补充解析（标志词与参考句式）：邀请的标志词 invite，同义替换 invitation；另有 On behalf of ..., I would like to invite you to ... 等。

:::

**写道歉信开头，该选哪句？**

### 第三组 · 展开与万能理由（段首句 / 过渡词 / 必备句型）

**finally / lastly / more importantly / last but not least 属于哪一类过渡词？**

- D. 称呼

::: details 点开核对答案

**Which sentence follows 句型一「最高级价值」？**

**Which sentence follows 句型三「产生影响」？**

### 第四组 · 分类型结尾与改错

**建议信的结尾该用哪句？**

- A. I am looking forward to seeing you.
- B. I hope you will take my suggestions into account.
- C. I am sorry for causing you inconvenience.
- D. We wish to share your honor and joy.

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（I hope you will take my suggestions into account.）** —— 笔记补充解析（不同类型书信的结尾）：建议信结尾另有 It is my earnest hope that my suggestions can be accepted. / I sincerely hope that these proposals are useful for your decision-making.。

**邀请信的结尾该用哪句？**

- A. It is my earnest hope that my suggestions can be accepted.
- B. Please confirm your participation at your earliest convenience.
- C. I hope you will forgive my mistake which I terribly regret.
- D. Should you give me a trial, I would do my utmost.

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：B（Please confirm your participation at your earliest convenience.）** —— 笔记补充解析（不同类型书信的结尾）：邀请信结尾为 I am looking forward to seeing you. / Please confirm your participation at your earliest convenience. / We would appreciate it if you could honor us with your presence.。

:::

**道歉信的结尾该用哪句？**

- A. Your prompt attention to this letter would be highly appreciated.
- B. I wish you success and fulfillment in the future.
- C. I am sorry for causing you inconvenience.
- D. Please consider me for your advertised position.

::: details 点开核对答案

**答案：C（I am sorry for causing you inconvenience.）** —— 笔记补充解析（不同类型书信的结尾）：道歉信结尾为 I am sorry for causing you inconvenience. / I hope you will forgive my mistake which I terribly regret. / I sincerely hope that you will be able to think in my position and accept my apology.。

:::

**求职信的结尾该用哪句？**

- A. I am looking forward to your prompt reply.
- B. We wish to share your honor and joy.
- C. I shall be much obliged if you accord me an opportunity for an interview.
- D. Please accept my sincere apology.

::: details 点开核对答案

:::

**询问信（期待回信）的结尾该用哪句？**

:::

**祝贺信的结尾该用哪句？**

:::

**第三段「重申主题与寒暄」的套话是哪句？**
